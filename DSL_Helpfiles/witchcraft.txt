WITCHCRAFT
'WITCHCRAFT SPELLS'

A collection of hexes and spells known only to witches and warlocks.  These
spells can be devilish indeed, or simply entertaining in the right hands.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The witchcraft spell group consists of the following spells:

create cauldron         creates a cauldron for brewing
find familiar           locates a familiar for the caster
silence                 prevents spell casting and talking by target
possess familiar        allows witch/warlock to posses their familiar
fear                    causes enemy to flee in terror
splinter                causes wooden items to splinter into stakes

This group is available to the following classes -
    WITCH/WARLOCK
