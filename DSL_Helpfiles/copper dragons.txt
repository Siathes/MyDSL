'COPPER DRAGONS'
Perhaps the most selfish of good dragons, copper dragons are still very
good-hearted. They easily give in to arguments of others, and often
consult with other dragons before making important decisions. They are
loyal to their friends and distrust strangers. Although copper dragons are
the smallest of the metallic dragons, they command magic well, are crafty
fighters, and excellent jumpers. They lair in rocky, mountainous regions.
Copper dragons resist fighting, attacking only when creatures dear to them
are in jeopardy. In any combat situation, copper dragons begin their
assult by breathing a cloud of slowness gas, and followed up with any
non-damaging spells they know. If their attackers won't go away, coppers
can always breathe a stream of acid on them.
