DRAGONS

 Dragons are unchallenged as the most powerful children of our world.  These
massive reptiles are covered with thick scales from their tail-tips to their
toothy mouths.  They have wings strong enough to bear them aloft and to
produce a blast of air.  The ten greater dragon types of our world belong to
two families: the evil chromatic dragons (black, blue, green, red, white and
brown) and the good metallic dragons (brass, bronze, copper, gold, silver
and steel).  Dragons grow larger, and their power greater, with age.

 When dragons fight, they claw, bite, kick, slap their tails, and buffet
dragon species has their own specialty breath.  Dragons are smart enough to
use their more dangerous attacks against their more dangerous foes or when
they face many enemies at once.   All dragons have the potential for
learning these spellgroups and skills: For help specific to each type of
dragon see:

'BLACK DRAGON' 'BLUE DRAGON' 'GREEN DRAGON' 'RED DRAGON' 'WHITE DRAGON'
'BRASS DRAGON' 'BRONZE DRAGON' 'COPPER DRAGON' 'GOLD DRAGON' 'SILVER DRAGON'
'BROWN DRAGON' 'STEEL DRAGON' 'CRYSTAL DRAGON' 'TOPAZ DRAGON'

Dragon Breath and Chamber:

 Dragons also have the ability to chamber their breath until such a time
that they wish to unleash it upon their enemies.  This specialized attack
can either be directly targeted or delivered as an area attack.  The dragon
has the ability to vary the strength of its exhaled breath each time it uses
it though as this attack draws directly from the dragon's natural abilities.
When the dragon's air is exhausted, the dragon will need to recover over
time.

Vitality and Death:

Dragons will lose vitality with every death though not alterforms.  When
a dragon's vitality is gone, the dragon will permanently die.  Dragons and
the players behind them are held to the HIGHEST of standards.  If you cheat,
fail to follow all game rules, get penalized for any reason, or fail to meet
role-playing standards, your Dragon character will be denied instantly.
Playing a Dragon is not meant to be easy, nor fair.  They can be very
powerful indeed and it will take a dedicated player to have one long term.
