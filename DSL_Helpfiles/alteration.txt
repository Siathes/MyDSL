ALTERATION 'ALTERATION SPELLS'

The alteration spell group contains numerous spells that allow one to
literally change, or alter themselves, or items around them, either
temporarily, or permanently.  This group is extremely specialized, and as
such, is available only to those members of the conclave that are
transmuters.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The alteration spell group consists of the following spells:

alter armor        changes armor from one type into cloth
alter elements     changes silver into gold
alter self         disguises player as a mob
blackstaff         transforms a special gem into a staff with exceptional properties
disjunction        a powerful disrupting spell which dispels magic as well as deals damage
enlarge            enlarges characters
forget             causes one's foe to forget critical spells and skills
frost shroud       chills an enemy and can freeze an enemies feet to the ground
permanency         changes a temporary effect to a permanent one, has side-effects
reduce             reduces the size of characters
summon manticore   summons a charmed beast of magic
wizard mark        marks an object as the property of the wizard who casts this

This group is available to the following classes -

    TRANSMUTERS
