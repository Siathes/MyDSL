WING STALL 'WING STALL'
Syntax:  wing stall
Wing stall is a skill known only to dragons. (Of course, who else has wings?
*grin*)  Dragons may close up their wings allowing them to plummet earthward
briefly, then stall by spreading their wings again.  The effect of this is to
stir up dust and debris, damaging every creature in the room.  While it may
seem like play to a dragon, it is interpreted by those affected as an attack.
