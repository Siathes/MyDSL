        Recommended Skills and Spells

    There are several spellgroups and skills that are worth taking on any
      character. Here is a brief overview on these skills.
    As a melee class, it is imperative that you take Second and Third
      Attack, Dual Wield, and Enhanced Damage. As a casting class, these
      skills wane in usefulness.
    Mage classes must take Combat and Beguiling spells. They don't hit
      very well, so they rely on charmed monsters and combat spells to do
      damage for them.
    Bards need to take Pugil, Second Attack, and Enhanced Damage in
      conjunction with War Hymns and Hymns of Life. These are all supplied
      in Bard Default, so just take that.
    All classes should take Dodge and Parry. Shield block is equally
      useful, but is not required if you are a melee class who plans on
      dual wielding.
    The Protective spellgroup is a necessity. Luckily, most classes have
      access to it. It provides the sanctuary spell and also protection
      vs. alignment.
    The Enhancement spellgroups offers the Haste spell, which is
      incredibly useful for melee classes. For mages, transmuters, and
      enchantors, it provides the Water Breath spell. Not as necessary as
      Protective, but it'll save you much money in the long run.
    The Detection spellgroup is a must if it is accessible. Many players
      have access to invis and sneak, so trying to communicate with
      people you cannot see will become annoying. Also, there are some
      MOBs that have invis and sneak.
    Finally, it is almost always fruitful to take your class's Default
      group. They provide most of your useful skills and spellgroups for
      a reduced price.
