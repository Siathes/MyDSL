PORTAL LOCATIONS -

Kingdoms and clans have the option of expanding their magical portals
within their respective halls, that open to different areas around the
realm, at a cost.  The following locations are the current areas that can be
requested for portal transportation.

Althainia Port
Arkane Port
Haon Dor
Icewall Port
Northwest Coast of Icewall
Tropica Port
