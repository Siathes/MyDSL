{cSyntax:{x Lotto Buy

The Algoron Lottery, started by Randorf Del'nichi, gives anyone willing to
buy a lottery ticket a chance at becoming a bit richer.

The game is simple.  You purchase a ticket for 1 egg and choose 2 numbers
between 1 and 100.  Every Tuesday and Saturday at noon system time the
winning numbers will be drawn.  The winner(s) take home 75% of the jackpot.
The kingdom(s) in which the winning ticket(s) were bought will get 15% of it
and I will take 10% for an administration fee.  If there is no winner the
jackpot rolls to the next drawing.  To clarify if more that one person wins
the 75% is split.

If you match only one number you will recieve 5 eggs.  This does not affect
the main pot.

Listed below are the current kingdoms where lottery tickets can be
purchased.  For kingdoms wishing to purchase a lottery ticket stall, the
Del'nichi family will extend licensing rights for 100 eggs per stall setup.

Nordmaar - Nordmaar Lottery Stall

Thaxanos - Thaxanos Lottery Office
Verminasia - Blood Money Lotto Store

To purchase a ticket(s), journey to the kingdom of your choice and speak
with the vendor located there.  You will need to reach out to kingdom
officials or residents for locations if you are not familiar with the
kingdom map.
