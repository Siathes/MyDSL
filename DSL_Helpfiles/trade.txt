TRADE
Trade is used to securely trade items between two players.  Both players
must accept the trade to complete it.  Any item or amount of coin added will
force both parties to accept again.  Once a trade has begun you are only
allowed to use a few commands otherwise the trade will be cancelled.  You
can use the say, speak, inventory, worth, score, and trade commands once a
trade has begun.

Syntax:
trade <item> <player>                  -Adds the item to the trade
trade <number>*<item> <player>         -Adds that number of items to the trade
trade <amount> <gold/silver> <player>  -Adds that much gold/silver to the trade
trade list                             -Displays what is being traded
trade cancel                           -Cancels the trade
trade accept                           -Accepts the trade
