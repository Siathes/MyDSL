ghost
When you die, you are turned into a ghost.  It is recommended that you
go and find your corpse and collect your belongings, which will be where you
last died.  Or you can wait for it to reappear after several moments at your
recall point.

IF you are a ghost you can NOT interfere in pk.  This means summoning
people, dropping potions, telling people where others are, closing/opening
doors.  Or anything else you can think of.  The only thing you should be
doing is getting your belongings, going home and waiting it out.
