Welcome to DSL!  Starting a new character and learning the environment of
a game as vast and evolving as is found in DSL, can be challenging as you
begin your journey anew but also well rewarding in the many opportunities
that can be found in either a kingdom or clanned (PKP) environment.

The staff of DSL wants to make sure that your playing experience here is a
pleasant one and is available to you should you have ideas or questions.  We
work hard to enhance the overall playing environment of DSL and while we
prefer our interactions to be through roleplay or offering competitive
player versus player events sometimes it is less pleasant as we need to
issue warning or punishment for rule breaking.

Some of the major rules are listed below and will familiarize you with our
expectations before you start your play and to assist you in avoiding issues
that might mar your DSL experience.  These are enforced by the imm staff and
a full listing and further explanations can be found by typing help rules.
Have fun and let your imagination take you away!

--DO NOT RUN SCRIPTS/TRIGGERS TO KEEP YOU IN GAME WHILE YOU ARE AWAY FROM
  YOUR COMPUTER (AFK) OR INATTENTIVE. (See Help Scripting)
--NO SWEARING OR LEWD BEHAVIOR ON ANY CHANNEL OR THE FORUMS (Rule 1a)
--NO MULTIPLAYING (Rule 4c or see Help Multiplaying)
--NO CLAN/KINGDOM INTERFERENCE OF ANY KIND (Rules 2 and 3)
--NO MOB SOFTING OR GROUPING OUT OF RANGE (Refer to Rule 6a)
--APPROPRIATE CHARACTER ROLE PLAY IS REQUIRED
--NO ROLEPLAY OF ANY RAPE, INCEST, CHILD SEX OR ANY OTHER ILLEGAL ACTIVITY
--NO ADVERTISING OF OTHER MUDS OR MASSIVE MULTIPLAYER GAMES
--DO NOT ALT REVEAL YOURSELF OR ANOTHER PERSON IN OR OUT OF THE GAME (Rule 4b)
--ALL CHARACTERS NEED TO BE ON A MASTER ACCOUNT.  ONLY 1 MASTER ACCOUNT PER
  PERSON.  (Rule 4d or see Help Master Accounts)

I acknowledge the above rules and their administration to ensure that
everyone has a fair and enjoyable playing experience.  While this is not a
complete list, I understand my responsibility to know and abide by the rules
and that Help Rules will give me a more comprehensive listing.
