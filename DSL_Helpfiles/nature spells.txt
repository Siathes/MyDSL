'NATURE SPELLS'

Only Rangers and Druids may learn this spell group and most of the spells
In it.  The nature spell group allows the caster to make use of the natural
world, and all things contained within it.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help <spellname>.
The nature spell group consists of the following spells:

bark skin            this spell turns the caster's skin to bark
call wild            calls wild animals to the aid of the ranger
create ranger staff  creates a weapon for the ranger
entangle             animates plants in the area to entangle a player
protection cold      protection from the effects of icy attacks
protection fire      protection from the effects of fiery attacks
swarm                this spell summons forth hundreds of biting insects
wrath of nature      an attack able to pierce through spell protection
nature growth      boosts the caster or its target in constitution
enhance seed         enhances the growing capability of a seed
