AGLBET

The AGL Betting system is for the AGL fans to place bets during the Algoron
Gladiator League matches.  After the match is announced, the person running
the match (typically Randorf or any of his drunken offspring) will announce
who is favored.  After that, you may go to the betting window and place your
bet.  You must be between 1 egg and 5, and based on the odds is how your
money will be returned upon winning.

Those of you with bad links, may not wish to bet.  If you drop link, your
bet is lost.

Syntax:  aglbet status

This will show you who is fighting and the odds once it has been announced.

Syntax: aglbet (contender) (amount in eggs)

     Example #1 - aglbet Scorn 3 (You bet 3 eggs on Scorn)
     Example #2 - aglbet 2 5 (You bet 5 eggs on Contender #2)

The eggs MUST be in your account to place a bet.
