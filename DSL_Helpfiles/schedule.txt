RULES FOR CLAN WARS XIX

No person can enter more than 1 character per night.  This means that all
clans should have 7 DIFFERENT players for any given night.  If this becomes
a problem, we'll look at allowing a few people to enter more than one event
per night mid-way through the season.

MATCH #1 - 1 vs 1 Normal Clan Wars match. Any level. AGL style rules apply.

MATCH #2 - 2 vs 2. AGL style rules apply. This is NOT tag format, both members
of the team will be fighting at the same time. The team with anyone still
standing is the winner.

MATCH #3 - 4 vs. 4 CAPTURE THE FLAG. ACFL Rules. Clan with worse record will
receive the benefit of substituting one member after lineups have been given.

Again, no Player (player, so this means you and YOUR alts) can not enter
more than 1 event per night.  All Clans should have/find/recruit 6 level
51's per night.
