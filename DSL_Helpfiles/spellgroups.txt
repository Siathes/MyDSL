SPELLGROUPS SPELLS

There are many different types of spells that are available to various
classes within DSL.  Most, if not all, may be grouped into various spell
groups that are offered for those classes to choose from.

Below is an index listing of the offered spell groups.  Typing HELP followed
by (most) of these groups, will give you a listing of the individual spells
within, and the classes they may be available to.

alteration              attack                  beguiling
benedictions            combat                  creation
curative                detection               divine blessings
elemental               enchantment             enhanced enchantment
enhancement             greater illusions       harmful
healing                 illusion                invocation
maladictions            nature                  necromancy
protective              transportation          voodoo
weather                 witchcraft              worship
