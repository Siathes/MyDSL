FATALE

Born of Necrucifer and Drakkara, Fatale is perhaps one of the most dangerous
Gods in the heavens.  Constantly practicing his technique, he is known to
have the most devoted and talented followers.

Fatale gained his position after posing as a mortal and decimating a temple
of Austinian and all of the followers within.  His weapon of choice is the
dagger, but he has been known to bless and enchant all other kinds of
weapons, tainting them with his evil blood.

When Fatale appears, it has been reported that he acts with the utmost
courtesy, beguiling the weak willed with his charm and wit.  Amazingly
attractive, his short, cropped black hair and piercing blue eyes captivate
audiences as he spins tales of murder and revenge.

Fatale also appears often with his brother Devion and when together pull
pranks on mortal-kind that generate laughter and applause from the dark
heavens.
