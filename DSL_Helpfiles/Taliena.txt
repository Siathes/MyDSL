TALIENA

With beauty to match that of Zandreya, Taliena is the devoted wife of
Austinian.  She was born of the rising sun and sent to this world to share
the warmth and happiness the day brings.  Her smile alone is said to cheer
up even the most unhappy people.

With flowing platinum hair and deep, caressing blue eyes, she represents
herself with grace and splendor.  Her followers tend to be female, and tend
to share her qualities and disposition.

She has been known to intervene in mortal affairs, especially those of
marital conflict.  Taliena has even been known to cheer up (or attempt to,
usually she has succeeded) people outside of her worship.  It is her desire
to see happiness and love permeate the world, and she wishes for an an end
to the day-to-day bloodshed.
