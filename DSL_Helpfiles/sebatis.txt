SEBATIS
A child-student of his father, Kwainin, Sebatis is the youngest of the
Neutral Gods.  But his youth by no means is representative of his power
over the Neutral magicks.  After studying for years with his father, he
was thrown into a conflict in the mortal realm, between the darkness and
the light.  Kwainin is said to have watched Sebatis closely in his final
test.
Sebatis didn't waver in his use of his magic and did not favor one side
over another.  He simply stood his ground and made sure the world was in
harmony and not plunged into Chaos.
He presides over the worshipers of Neutral magic.  And his followers
respect and adore him, oftentimes coming to blows when Sebatis' name is
used in a disrespectful tone.
When his appears to mortals, he looks incredibly child-like, yet strong
and determined.
