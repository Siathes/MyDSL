MENCIUS

Out of the darkness and madness of the heavens, Mencius, the God of
Vengeance and Rage was born.  Legend states that on the day of his birth,
the skies of the mortal world alit with lightning and continued to do so for
hours on end.  Reports say the sky was cloudless yet the angry lightning
struck.

The followers of Mencius tend to be volitile and completely untrustworthy.
Mencius watches over all his followers, and has been known to make pacts
with them in order to have his work carried out.

When he appears he does so wearing regal armor and always carrying a drawn
weapon.  He has been known to slay entire populations, razing towns
completely and stirring riots wherever he goes.  If you're not his devotee
and he appears to you, it has been advised to run away.
