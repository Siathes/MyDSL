        Searching For Players

    There are several commands to use to search other players online.
      These are who, whoc, whok, whois, and whoisic.
    Who, WhoC, and WhoK all have similar criteria searches. Typing who
      5 25 would search all players online between the levels of 5
      and 25. whoc 5 25 would do the same, but it would only search
      clanners and would display in an organized format that includes
      their rank. Possible criterias for these commands are to search by
      race, class, level, kingdom, and clan.
    WhoIs and WhoIsIC commands don't have search criterias. Use these
      commands to check on a certain player. Typing whois Bobby
      would display his general information like level, race, class,
      association, nobility, and title. Using whoisic Bobby would
      display his detailed information, including his background and
      description.
