HARVESTWOOD LUMBERJACK

This is a 1st tier craft that requires the apprentice only to work with
an appropriate saw and the lay of the land. You should find a shop with all
of the saws in any major city. Any of the higher level saws will work for
lower level wood but low level saws won't work for high level wood.  Also
your saws get weak as you use them and only last so long.  The higher
level saws wear out very quickly so it is best not to use them on low level
wood.  Also, the higher level saws may not yield the same amount of wood as
you search the thick forests.  Different types of trees are found in
different forests all around Algoron.

     Syntax: harvestwood <wood type>

           Wood/Saw Types            Location

  1 - 120  cedar/cedar saw         - Coven & Shi'ku Noria (Thieve's Forest)
120 - 240  fir/fir saw             - Forbidden Forest & Blood Sea
240 - 360  pine/pine saw           - Forbidden Forest & Yotanoko Trail
360 - 480  elm/elm saw             - Great Forest & White Moon Isle
480 - 600  oak/oak saw             - Yotanoko Trail & Shalonesti Forest
600 - 720  maple/maple saw         - Rainforest & Shalonesti Forest
720 - 840  hickory/hickory saw     - Shi'ku Noria & White Moon Isle
840 - 960  stonewood/stonewood saw - Great Forest & Rainforest
960 - 1000 ironwood/ironwood saw   - Shi'ku Noria & Rainforest
1000+      dreadwood/dreadwood saw - Great Forest & White Moon Isle
