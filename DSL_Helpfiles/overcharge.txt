OVERCHARGE

Once a spellcrafter fills a gem with essence it can sometimes be
overcharged by imparting the spellcrafters essence into the gem as well.
There is a decent chance of success but only superior quality diamond gems
will be able to endure the process.  Trying to overcharge a diamond of
standard quality will result in destroying the diamond.  The perfect rare
diamond, while able to provide the maximum effect upon an item, can also
create an unstable impact so that the item will rot upon the owners death.
For any diamond. regular or overcharged, you will not be able to place them
into an item until you reach legendary master status as a spellcrafter.

   Type of Essenced Gems                Effect               Points
---------------------------     ----------------------       ------
Magi and Weapon Flag Gems       Cannot be overcharged          --
Inertia/Life/Moon               Random +28 through +40        9-13
Growth/Age/Mind/Wind/Muscle     Random +9 through +13         9-13
Pain/Sight                      +3 Dam/Hit                     12

 Syntax: overcharge gem <essenced gem> or overcharge diamond <essenced gem>
 Example:  overcharge gem pain
