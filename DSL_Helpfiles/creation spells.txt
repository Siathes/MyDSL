'CREATION SPELLS'

The creation spell group is used to create objects, of temporary or
permanent duration.  Skill creators can travel without food or drink, and
use their powers to create nourishment as required.  All classes may become
learned in this group.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The creation spell group consists of the following spells:

continual light creates an eternal light source
create food     produces a nourishing mushroom
create rose     creates a beautiful red rose
create spring   calls forth a small but pure spring from the ground
create water    fills any available container with water
floating disc   creates a floating disc of force perfect for hauling treasure

This group is available to the following classes -
    ASSASSIN BATTLERAGER BLADESINGER CLERIC CRUSADER DRAGON
    DRUID GIANT INVOKER MAGE NECROMANCER PALADIN PRIEST RANGER
    SHADOWKNIGHT THIEF TRANSMUTER WARRIOR WITCH/WARLOCK
