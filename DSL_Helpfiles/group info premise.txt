GROUPS INFO PREMISE
The DSL skill system allows you to fully customize your new character, making
him or her skilled in the skills you choose.  But beware, the skills you pick
at character creation are the only skills you will ever learn.  Skills are
paid for with creation points, and the more creation points you have, the
harder it is to gain a level.  Furthermore, higher-cost skills are harder to
practice.
Skill groups are like package deals for characters -- sets of skills or spells
that are closely related, and hence can be learned as a unit.  There is a
default skill group for each class, which can be selected for a balanced
selection of skills at a somewhat reduced cost.
The experience breakdown (for humans of any class) is as follows:
            points   exp/level     points   exp/level
              40        1000          90       6000
              50        1500         100       8000
              60        2000         110      12000
              70        3000         120      16000
              80        4000         130      24000
The table continues in a similar manner for higher point totals.
Each race/class combination has a multiplier by which these values are
multiplied to determine the characters exp/level.  The multipliers range
from 1 to 2.5.  To find what yours is, make a character of the desired
race/class combination and find out what the exp/level is when the creation
points are at some multiple of 10 that's over 40.  Divide that exp/level by
the exp/level for the same number of creation points shown in the table and
you'll know what the multiplier is.  This should aid in your determination
of the number of exp/level your character will be, even after adding 3 cp for
a reclass.  It should be noted that for some race/class combinations, the
race/reclass combination will actually have a lower multiplier.
Use INFO to find out what is included in spell groups and defaults for races
and classes.  Example:  info ranger. . . lists all the default spellgroups and
skills for the ranger reclass.
