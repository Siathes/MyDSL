DESCRIPTION

All players are required to have a suitable description for their characters
which should be in place by level 5.  Descriptions should not be any less
than four (4) lines of text in written, paragraph form.

If players pass level 5 and still do not have a written description for
their character, they will be transed to the DSL Classroom where they can
work on their description and see examples of descriptions for their race
and others, should they have writers block.  These examples are NOT to be
used as a copy and paste but to give you ideas on how to write your own.
Descriptions can be changed or added to at any point in time.

The syntaxes below will assist you in creating your descriptions either
within the editor or line by line.  Please refer to the HELP EDITOR file
before entering the editor for a quick tutorial.

  Syntax: Description <string>            For use outside of the Editor
  Syntax: Description + <string>          For use outside of the Editor
  Syntax: Description - <string>          For use outside of the Editor
  Syntax: Desc ++                         Puts you in the Editor

Description <string> will set your long description to the given string that
you type in.  Using description + or - <string> will add or remove single
lines from your description.  To add or remove spacing with the + or -
feature, do not include any other text as your string.  Desc ++ will place
you into the editor where you can copy and paste paragraphs of text or free
write your own.  There are commands within the editor for centering,
formatting, etc.  So it is recommended that you review the HELP EDITOR
helpfile prior to using.

WHAT NOT TO DO:

- Include actions, thoughts, feelings, or ASCII art
- Tell other people how they react to your character
- Share stories about your features or equipment
- Combine your background with your description

NOT PERMITTED:  ASCII art or Excessive use of color (ie: kender colors)


WHAT TO DO:

- Use your creation characteristics as an outline
- Keep in line with your race and class
     - A priest can be burly, but will still wear cloth.
     - Kender are kender-sized, ogres are ogre-sized.
- Make the description flow
     - Start from the top or bottom and work to the other end
     - Does something about you immediately stand out?
- Consider what people would see and they would not
     - Is your dress green? We can see that.
     - Is your dagger hidden? We can't see that.
- Remember that small details can tell a lot about a person
     - Callused hands can imply physical labor.
     - Worn boots might mean that someone is well-travelled.
     - Eyes are the most important part of the face. Don't forget them.
- Dress your character appropriately for their class and position
     - Does your knight wear plate armor? Is it ornamental?
     - Is your dress modest or made from expensive material?
