'HARMFUL SPELLS'

This spell group is the opposite of healing.  The harmful spells are
designed to tear flesh from bone, rupture arteries, and generally turn the
body against itself.  Only bards, clerical, and warrior types may use this
spell group.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The harmful spell group, in order of power, consists of the
following spells:

cause light     inflicts minor wounds on an enemy
cause serious   inflicts wounds on a foe
cause critical  causes major damage to the target
cause decay     inflicts high damage, and possible maladictions
harm            a very deadly harmful spell
cause fatality  the most powerful harmful spell, possible instant death

This group is available to the following classes -
    BARD BATTLERAGER CLERIC CRUSADER DRAGON DRUID
    GIANT PALADIN PRIEST RANGER SHADOWKNIGHT WARRIOR
