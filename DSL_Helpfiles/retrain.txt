RETRAIN

syntax:  retrain <character password> <base class to retrain to>

You may choose to retrain at any time, whether you are currently reclassed
or not.  You will keep your race, sex, alignment, and merit or flaw if you
have one.  You will also keep your description and background, as well as
any title you have bestowed.  You will keep all questing points, eggs and
other monies as well.  You will also keep all hours you currently have
(thus, if you have 1000 hours when you retrain, you will still have 1000
hours after you've retrained).

The purpose of retraining is so that you may choose a new class.  When you
retrain, you will be placed into creation mode, just as if you are creating
a new character.  You will reroll your stats, and begin again at level 1.
Rules on equipment do apply, so if you retrain, you will lose any equipment
that you carry that is more than two levels above level 1.

Remorts may not retrain.

If you wish to retrain, seek out the guild master of your current class and
type the required syntax to retrain.
