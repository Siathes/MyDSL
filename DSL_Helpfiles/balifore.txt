BALIFORE

 Once known as the Kender Capital of the World, the kingdom of Balifore
contained buildings that appeared to be destroyed, half built, or built by
twenty different architects as a result of the kenders' natural tendency to
wander and not complete a task.  It's leadership took the title of King or
Queen and allowed both kender and gnomes to register for citizenry.

Today, the kender kingdom lay in scorched ruin in the southwestern part of
the Arkanian continent as result of Malachive and his Abhorrant, Deathmeer's
planned path of destruction.  A solitary shrine of Malachive stands among
the charred and ashen desolation, a symbol to all of the fallen God Child's
treachery upon the realm.
