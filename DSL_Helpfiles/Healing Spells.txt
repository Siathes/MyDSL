'HEALING SPELLS'

The healing spells are used to cure the wounds that adventurers inevitably
suffer in battle.  For curing other conditions, such as poison, see the
curative spell group.  Only warriors and clerics have access to this group.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The healing spell group, in order of power, consists of the
following spells:

cure light      heals minor wounds
refresh         restores vigor to a tired adventurer
cure serious    heals more severe wounds
cure critical   closes all but the worst wounds
heal            the most powerful healing spell
mass healing    casts a heal spell on every character in the room

This group is available to the following classes -
    ASSASSIN BATTLERAGER CLERIC CRUSADER DRAGON
    GIANT DRUID PALADIN PRIEST SHADOWKNIGHT WARRIOR
