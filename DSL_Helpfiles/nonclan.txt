NONCLAN
Groups that are not in the realms of pkilling are called non-clanned
groups. Such existing groups are the Austinians and the Marauders.
Kingdoms also follow into this category. They have the same benefits
as clanners (or will) with the exception of player killing and certain
pk related quests. If you want to form your own non-clan group, you
should note or email Scorn, submitting your idea in detail.
