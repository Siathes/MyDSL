BULKTRANSFER
This command allows you to give items to a persomore that one at a time.
It will allow you to give them up to thier item capacity or twice thier
weight capacity whichever is reached first.  This can only be used in the
vault room.  Once your are over your weight capacity you willnot be able to
move until you get back under it.

Syntax: bulktransfer <item count> <object name> <character name>
