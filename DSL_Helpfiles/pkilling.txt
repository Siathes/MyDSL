PKILLING PKILLER 'PLAYER KILLING' PSTEALING 'PLAYER STEALING'
Player killing and player stealing are allowed to all clan members
(including loners and renegades), provided the victim is within 8 levels of
the character and is also in a clan.  This will flag you as WANTED, allowing
any character to attack without level restrictions, and also provoking
action from various guards in the cities of Algoron.
