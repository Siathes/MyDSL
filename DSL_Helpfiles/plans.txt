change the entire world. A person in the right place at the right time can
change everything.  Army's of Good & Evil can conquer Kingdoms, Nations and
cities.  Right now, there are too many changes to list, but here are some.
New Reclasses:
Jongleurs and Swashbucklers are now in.
We have extended out reclass to list to over 50 new reclasses, all with their own
original skills or spells. A list for these will not be made public until they are
being developed.
Areas:
We will be filling in the Icewall and Tropica continents with original areas.
Kingdom Battles:
Battlefield Sim is being developed into an online program viewed via the Web Page.
Until then, kingdom battles are on hold.
Kingdom Mutinies:
They must be done as a military takeover. You MUST contact Grokle or Kanoir
and tell them that you are actively going to purchase an army to takeover
the kingdom. Failure to keep striving for this will result in us giving up
on keeping up with your military purchases.
