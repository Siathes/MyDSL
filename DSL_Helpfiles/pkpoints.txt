PKP - PLAYER KILL POINTS

Clanners get PK Points for every kill obtained in their group as long as
its in the same room as the person making the kill and they have not killed
that character in the last 4 kills.  You also get one for dying to another
player.

Syntax:  PKP - Will show you your stat allocation information
Syntax:  PkpTrain <stat> - Will train the specified stat
Syntax:  Pkptrain reset - Will reset your pkp - Must type it twice
         Cost to reset PKP: 10 Eggs

With each PK level, you can train 1 pkstat.  PKStr, PKInt, PKWis, PKDex,
PKCon.  These work as bonues on top of their regular stats.  These DO NOT
work in any room flagged ARENA.  That keeps the AGL, ACFL and other things
fair between clanners and non clanners.

Each stat can be trained up to +12 (three brackets for everyone regardless
of how your race/classes stats are allocated.)  After the 12th stat,
if will cost 4 times as much.  So if you want to go from 12 to 13 STR,
it will cost 4 pkp trains (or you could go +4 into a different uncapped stat)

For races that already have more than the 300 total stat, they will need to
reach a furthur level to be able to train.

For example: Trolls have a total max stat of 320.  They will have to reach
PK-Level 21 to train their first stat.  Kender have a total max stat of 273,
so they will get trains starting at PK level 1.  (As would any race 300 or
below)
