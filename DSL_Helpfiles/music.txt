MUSIC
Syntax:  music <lyrics>
MUSIC is the command for communicating on the music channel.  There
are no requirements that your communications on the music channel
have to be in character, but it's nice to see that a few bards have
shared their talents with the playerbase via this channel.
The second form of the syntax, where no argument is used with the
command, acts as a toggle to turn the music channel on and off.
See also:  CHANNELS
