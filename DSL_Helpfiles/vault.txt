VAULT
The mystic vault is a great discovery.  You can access this vault from many
locations.  You can use it to store large amounts of items.  The vault can
tell who is who and only allows you access to your own items.  The vault
acts like any other container but you can't take it with you.  There is no
need to open the vault just put in or take out you need.

The vault rules are as follows:
- It costs 1 egg to open your account. That account will remain open
  indefinately.  To open your account type register in the vault room.
- You may put up to 20 types of items in the vault.
- You may put up to 1000 of each type of item in the vault.
- You may put items above your level in the vault but you will not be
  able to retreive them until you reach that level.
- You may not put any money or gems in the vault.
- You may not put containers in the vault if they contain other items.
