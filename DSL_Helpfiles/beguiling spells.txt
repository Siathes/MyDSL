'BEGUILING SPELLS'

This insidious spell group is used to invade the minds of others.  It
consists of various charming magics, well suited for turning your foes to
your cause.  Generally, only mages and thieves have access to these spells.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The beguiling spell group consists of the following spells:

calm            if successful, stops all fighting in the room
charm person    turns an enemy into a trusted friend
sleep           puts a foe into enchanted slumber
restore mind    causes a foe to regain its free will
betray          causes a charmed enemy to betray thier master and join you

This group is available to the following classes:
    DRAGON MAGE THIEF TRANSMUTER
