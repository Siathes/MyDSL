DRAGOTH

Born of Necrucifer and Drakkara, Dragoth claims control of disease and
decay.  After learning the black magicks from his mother, Drakkara, Dragoth
concentrated his study on sicknesses and plagues.  He applauds the use of
disease and decay in battle, and responds especially to murders caused by
disease.

His study has caused a transformation of self, disfiguring the face, and
causing him to appear hooded at all times.  He has also been known to cough
uncontrollably, his fits causing whole rooms to be contaminated with his
poisonous ways.

Dragoth is not to be taken lightly, his diseases and poisons are the most
powerful in the land, and his followers have learned from Dragoth how to
apply the art of disease upon their unsuspecting victims.
