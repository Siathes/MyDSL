SHEATH UNSHEATH
Syntax:  sheath <weapon>
         unsheath <weapon>
Sheath allows you to carry a spare weapon in a 'sheath'.  You are so
familiar with the location of the sheath, that you can even access it while
blinded.
Wield <weapon> will take a weapon from the sheath or your inventory, and wield
it as a primary weapon.
Second <weapon> will take a weapon from your sheath or inventory, and wield it
as a secondary weapon (if you have dual wield).
