swashbuckler
These care-free dashing warriors glide through life with grace, wit and
charm.  Disdainful of most common weapons, they are extremely proficient at
those of their choosing.

Ever ready for adventure, swashbucklers have a multitude of skills at their
disposal to help them on their way.  Once they have advanced far enough in
their training it is rumored that they are able to don a mask to hide their
true identities.

Who can be a swashbuckler?

CLASS: Warriors only
RACE: Humans, half-elves and kender

See Other Helps: MASK, BOARDING, EVASION, MIMIC, RETAIN WEAPON,
VIOLENT DISPOSSESSION, TARGET, OCEAN CALL
