'BENEDICTIONS SPELLS'

Benedictions are priestly spells, used to bestow divine favor upon your
allies.  They are often restricted to those of like alignment.  Only
clerical and warrior types may use these spells.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The benedictions spell group consists of the following spells:

bless           bestows divine favor upon the target or target item
calm            if successful, stops all fighting in the room
frenzy          places the recipient into a berserker rage
holy word       aids your allies while calling divine wrath upon your foes
remove curse    removes malevolent magic from players and items
know religion   finds one's spiritual deity

This group is available to the following classes -
    BATTLERAGER CLERIC CRUSADER DRAGON DRUID GIANT
    PALADIN PRIEST SHADOWKNIGHT SHAMAN WARRIOR
