TALON
[ Talon ]

Talon is a self serving militant order of trained soldiers.  They have
patterned their lore and fighting styles after that of Dragons and Dragons
as a whole are envied by the Order.  Contrary to what some believe, Talon
are NOT the protectors of Dragons.  The Order of the Talon revere Dragons
but know as well as anyone that Dragons do not need protecting and some need
to be destroyed.

Members of the Order of the Talon are the most skilled Dragon Riders in the
world.  Already fierce in combat, Talon's might is also enhanced by the fact
that they reserve one constant spot of membership of their order for a
Dragon of their choosing.  This Dragon is replaced by another anytime the
Highlord orders it.  (And the Dragon is willing of course)

Talon is divided into 3 factions of the clan, all sorted by alignment.

Fang (Evil Members) Claw (Good Members) Scale (Neutral Members)

All 3 orders have a rank hierarchy which is controlled by the Orders
Highlord and seconded by the Clans Lord or Lady.
