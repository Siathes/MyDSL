CITIZENSHIP
If you would like to become a citizen of one of the kingdoms, seek
out anyone from that kingdom who has a royalty flag (KING, QUEEN, PRINCE,
PRINCESS) and speak with them.  These people have functions much like
those of clan recruiters and can make requests that the immortals flag
you as a citizen of their kingdom.
Citizenship currently is used to add to the roleplay on DSL and does not
have any game effects yet.
