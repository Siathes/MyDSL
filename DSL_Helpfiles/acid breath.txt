'ACID BREATH' 'FIRE BREATH' 'FROST BREATH' 'GAS BREATH' 'LIGHTNING BREATH'

These spells are for the use of dragons.  Acid and lightning damage one
victim, whereas gas, fire, and frost damage every creature/PC in the room.
Fire and frost can break objects, lightning can melt jewelry, gas poisons
food and drink, and acid can impact how well your armor offers protection.

Syntax:  cast 'lightning breath'  <victim>
Syntax:  cast 'acid breath'       <victim>
Syntax:  cast 'fire breath'       <victim>
Syntax:  cast 'frost breath'      <victim>
Syntax:  cast 'gas breath'        <victim>
