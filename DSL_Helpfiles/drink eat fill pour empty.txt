DRINK EAT FILL POUR EMPTY

Syntax: drink <object>
Syntax: eat   <object>
Syntax: fill  <object>
Syntax: pour  <object> out
Syntax: pour  <object> <object>
Syntax: empty <object>
Syntax: empty <object> <object>

When you are thirsty, DRINK something.

When you are hungry, EAT something.

Players that are level 5 and below are not affected by the eat/drink code, however, any
level over 5 that fails to eat/drink for a prolonged period of time when they become
hungry/thirsty will begin to have fainting spells and continually fall asleep until
they finally eat, drink and then rest.

FILL fills a drink container with water or other liquid.

POUR transfers a liquid to a container or empties one. You can also pour
from an object into something a character is holding.

EMPTY is used to either empty a container or transfer items to another
container.
