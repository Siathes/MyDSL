CHURCHES

The priests of Algoron are responsible for the leadership and management of
the church of their chosen god or goddess.  While any class may be a part of
their gods or goddesss church (clerics oftentimes serve as acolytes), only
priests will be granted the authority of leadership within each church.

Leadership is based on a hierarchical system.  Generally, the churchs leader
is chosen from the highest ranking priests, although exceptions have
occurred in rare instances when extenuating circumstances demand otherwise.
Priests gain more responsibility within their church as their rank
increases, showing the proper respect toward those of higher rank.

The church leader is responsible for tracking the members of his church and
ensuring his god or goddess is informed of their status at all times.  If a
church leader believes a priest or priestess deserves a promotion, he is
responsible for making his recommendation known.  The church leader must
also ensure that those immediately beneath him are able to carry on this
task should he be unable to do so.  Each church leader may organize his
church at his own discretion, based on either his knowledge of his gods
wishes or direct guidance from his god.  However, failure to perform ones
duty can result in harsh penalties.
