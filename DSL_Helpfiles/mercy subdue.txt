MERCY SUBDUE
Mercy and subdue are two commands that affect the outcome of a player
battle.  If subdue is toggle on, when you are about to be pkilled, you will
grovel for mercy.  If the victor of the fight has 'mercy' toggled on, he
will grant the loser their life.  Either way, an embarassing message will be
broadcast to all within the clan system that you begged for mercy.
