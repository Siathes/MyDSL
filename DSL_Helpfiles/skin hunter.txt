SKIN HUNTER

This is a 1st tier craft that requires the apprentice only to work with
an appropriate knife and the wild life that inhabits Algoron.  You should
find a shop with all of the knives in any major city.  Any of the higher
level knives will work for lower level skins but low level knives won't
work for high level skins.  Also your knives get weak as you use them and
only last so long.  The higher level knives wear out very quickly so it
is best not to use them on low level skins.  Also, the higher level knives
may not yield the same amount of skins as you search the wild.  Animals
with skins useful for this craft are found scattered across Algoron.  You
are not required to have an animal only to be in the correct area.

     Syntax: skin <animal type>
