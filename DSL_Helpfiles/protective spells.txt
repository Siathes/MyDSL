'PROTECTIVE SPELLS'

The protective spells are used to shield against harm, whether from spells
or physical attack.  They range from the weak armor spell to the
sought-after sanctuary charm.  Also included in this group are several
spells for dispelling hostile magics.  Any class may use this group.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The protective spell group consists of the following spells:

armor              provides the target with an extra layer of defense
cancellation       a powerful dispel, used for removing spells from friends
dispel magic       removes spells from enemies, not as effective as cancel
fireproof          shields items from the harmful effects of fire and acid
protection evil    provides defense from the attacks of evil creatures
protection good    protects from the attacks of good beings
protection neutral protects from the attacks of neutral entities
sanctuary          reduces all damage taken by the recipient by half
shield             puts a shimmering shield between you and your enemies
stoneskin          turns skin hard as stone, providing a huge armor boost

This group is available to the following classes -
    ASSASSINS BARDS BATTLERAGER BLADESINGER CLERIC CRUSADER
    DRAGON DRUID ENCHANTOR GIANT ILLUSIONIST INVOKER MAGE
    NECROMANCER PALADIN PRIEST SHADOWKNIGHT SHAMAN RANGER
    THIEF TRANSMUTER WARRIOR WITCH/WARLOCK
