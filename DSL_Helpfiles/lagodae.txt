LAGODAE

Lagodae, the females of the lapine race, are somewhat larger than a human,
which seems an odd contrast in comparison to their smaller counterparts, the
Lepori.  Strong, although strangely not terribly tough, Lagodae are swift of
foot and possess a devastating strength in their legs, allowing them to leap
distances that would daunt a gymnast.  That strength can also be turned
toward their enemies, giving would-be interlopers in the burrows or threats
to their kin a fierce reckoning should it be required.  Their furred forms
tend toward lean and strong, with long ears that are either upright or
lopped and large eyes that allow for good vision underground.  Their tails
are stubby, more in line with common rabbits, while their feet are larger
than most mortals', allowing for their fleetness of foot and granting them
great feats of acrobatics.
