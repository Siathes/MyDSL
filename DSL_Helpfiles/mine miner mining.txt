{YMINE MINER MINING{x

This is a 1st tier craft that requires the apprentice only to work with
an appropriate pickaxe and the lay of the land.  You should find a shop
with all of the picks in any major city. Any of the higher level picks will
work for lower level ore but low level picks won't work for high level ore.
Also your picks get weak as you use them and only last so long.  The higher
level picks wear out very quickly so it is best not to use them on low
level ore.  Also, the higher level picks may not yield the same amount of
ore as you search the rugged terrains.  Different ores are found in
different locations mostly in the hills and mountains.

     {cSyntax:{x mine <ore type>
