'ILLUSION SPELLS'

The illusions spells are dedicated to deception and trickery.  They can be
used to mask appearances or create distractions for the party.  Currently,
the illusion group is very small, but it may be expanded in the future.
Only thieves and mages can cast illusion spells.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The illusions spell group consists of the following spells:

invis           turns the target invisible
mass invis      turns the caster's group invisible
ventriloquate   allows the caster to put words in someone's mouth
self projection shifts an image of oneself before them

This group is available to the following classes -
    BLADESINGER DRAGON GIANT INVOKER MAGE
    NECROMANCER THIEF TRANSMUTER ASSASSIN
