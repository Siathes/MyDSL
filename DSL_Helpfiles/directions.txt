DIRECTIONS

Syntax: North  or  N          Syntax: Northeast   or   NE
Syntax: South  or  S          Syntax: Northwest   or   NW
Syntax: East   or  E          Syntax: Southeast   or   SE
Syntax: West   or  W          Syntax: Southwest   or   SW
Syntax: Up     or  U
Syntax: Down   or  D

Use these commands to walk in a particular direction.  If you see an exit
but the mud will not allow you to walk in that direction there are many
possible reasons.  Some of the reasons include: you're exhausted (out of
movement), you can't fly or swim, you don't have permission to enter the
room in that direction (it's a clan hall and you're not a member, or it might
be a privately owned home), the room may be restricted to certain races or
levels of characters, or the entrance might be to small for you to squeeze
through with all your gear.
