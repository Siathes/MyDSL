CLAN OUTPOST

A clan can fund and build an outpost on another continent if they so wish
for the cost of 750 eggs.  What this entitles them to is 2 rooms connected
to a kingdom or area near a kingdom that offers shelter and safety.  If they
want furniture you have to buy it.  If you want regen, portals - including a
portal to your clanhall, a healer, etc.  These are all extra costs.

What is required is permission from the Kingdom hosting your outpost.  Only
1 outpost is permitted per kingdom.  Meaning if Justice has an outpost in
Althainia, then no one else can have an outpost in Althainia.  The kingdom
will also need to agree to and pay an upfront building tax of 250 eggs
for the occupation of an outpost in their kingdom.

The host kingdom also reserves the right to destroy the outpost if they so
choose.

You also have to show extensive rp reasoning for the outposts existence and
reason for being there, on both sides from the clan and the kingdom.

CLAN PRICING:

Outpost Building ...................750 Eggs for two rooms and one exit
Room Remodel ....................... 40 Eggs per room - Remodel Only
Object in Outpost (furniture) ...... 20 Eggs per object
Nighttime Description .............. 20 Eggs per room - New or Remodel
Extended Descriptions .............. 10 Eggs per extended
Regen Rate Increase ................ 15 Eggs per 10% boost for hp or mana - up to 200%
Healer Employment ..................250 Eggs and starts at level 35
Increase Healer Level .............. 50 Eggs per level up to level 51
Outpost Portals .......... ........ 100 Eggs per portal - Portals only go to specific locations

KINGDOM COSTS:

Outpost Building Tax ................250 Eggs for the initial build
Destroy Clan Outpost .............. 1000 Eggs
