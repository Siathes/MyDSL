DISABLE ENABLE
Syntax:  disable <name>
         enable <name>
Disables the named person from sending you tells, though if you
were the last person to send them a tell, they will still be able
to reply.  You can not send tells to anyone whom you've disabled.
Disabling only remains in effect for the current session, if you
log out, or the system is rebooted, you will need to disable the
player again if you still don't want to receive tells from them.
You may receive tells from them again by typing enable <name>.
This is currently broken.  At the time of writing this helpfile,
disable did not disable receipt of tells, but did disable your
ability to send them.  Enable was also not working, requiring you
to quit and log back in if you wished to be able to send tells to
the person whom you disabled.
