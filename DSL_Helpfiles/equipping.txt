Equipment Most items later on will have additional attributes, or
"flags," added to them.  The DSL Equipment List can be found on the DSL
website at http://www.dsl-mud.org/algoron/equipment.asp.  You may also
use the identify command You can check your current equipment with
equipment.  Look at other peoples uipment by using look (person).

Hit - This stat adds to your hitroll, which decides how accurate your hits
will be.  Very important to melee classes like warrior, ranger, or thief.

Dam - This stat adds to your damroll.  Also very important for melee
classes.

Saves - Magical resistence.  The lower your saves, the more likely you are
to resist a spell.

AC - Armor Class.  Reduces damage taken very slightly.  The lower your AC,
the better.

Vampiric - Weapon flag that absorbs health with each strike.

Flaming, Freezing, Shocking - Weapon flags that add additional elemental
attacks to each strike.

Bless and Curse - The item is permanently blessed or cursed.  Curses can be
removed by the "remove curse" spell, but will come back unless a priest
casts "dispel curse" on it.  Also, all weapons have "nouns" like slash,
crush, stab, and flaming bite.  Some MOBs and races have vulnerabilities to
certain nouns.  For example, yinn are vulnerable to fire and resistant to
cold, so a weapon that has a "flaming bite" will do more damage to them
while "freezing bite" would do less.  Also, shocking weapons will do more
damage to anything when fighting underwater.
