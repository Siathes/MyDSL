PIRATES

Pirates are the scourge of the sea, living from battle to battle in their
search for treasure.  Pirates are unique in their ability to wield firearms
and incendiary devices. Because of the havoc they wreak on land and sea, the
longer a pirate stalks the realm, the bigger his reputation.

There are six levels of pirate reputation:
Cabin Person
Pirate
Renown Pirate
Dread Pirate
Notorious Pirate
Captain

The following races may not be pirates:

Shalonesti Elves, Pixie, Giant Ogres,
Mountain & Hill Dwarves, Centaurs and Ariel.

You can only become a Pirate by reclassing from the thief class.
