{YMALEDICTION SPELLS{x

Maledictions are a group of curses and other baneful spells, designed to
cripple, inconvenience, or torture, rather than kill outright.  These spells
may be cast by any class.  Please note that for info purposes, this is
currently spelled maladictions.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help (spellname).
The malediction spell group consists of the following spells:

     {yblindness{x       strikes the target blind
     {ychange sex{x      changes the sex of the target
     {ycurse{x           prevents recalling and weakens the target in combat
     {yenergy drain{x    drains experience and mana, while strengthening the caster
     {yheart blight{x    afflicts the target with a damaging disease
     {yplague{x          causes the target to suffer a slow, painful death from plague
     {ypoison{x          weaker than plague, and a constant, waking irritation
     {yslow}x           slows your enemies down, reducing their rate of attack
     {yweaken{x          drains the strength of the target
