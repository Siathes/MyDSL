VERMINASIA

Verminasia, the eloquent Kingdom of evil.

Once its streets were hailed as the most wondrous in all of Algoron.  Now
its former glory has gone.  Yet all the delights and hidden charm remain,
its streets may not be paved with gold anymore, but they still harbor stores
where lovers charms and fine restaurants are the norm.  This non-clanned
realm is ruled by a King and Queen, and is home to all the followers of the
dark gods.  Those of neutral beliefs may also find a home within this
kingdom.

Citizens may seek a position within the magical and clerical orders or seek
a place in Verminasia's military.  Race and sex have no meaning here, rank
and privilege given out on merits alone.  Verminasia strives to regain what
once was and spread the shadow of the dark gods across Algoron, at the head
of the Aversian Empire, by any means necessary.  Verminasia stands a bastion
in a world of light, but even the sun casts shadows.
