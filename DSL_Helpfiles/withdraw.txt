WITHDRAW
Syntax:  withdraw <number>
         withdraw <number> blue    -to witdraw blue diaminds instead of eggs

This command allows you to withdraw a number of jeweled eggs
from a bank.  You can not withdraw more jeweled eggs than you
have deposited.  To find out the number of eggs you have in your
account, look closely at your score.
See also:  DEPOSIT, BANK, MONEY, SCORE
