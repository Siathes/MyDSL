DRAGONSKIN ARMOR

Legendary Grand Master Armorcrafters and Tailors are able to craft any
kind of armor from a dragonskin.  In addition to superb armor class this
armor also retains some of its increased affectiveness against certain types
of attacks.  Note that one piece of armor will not protecte your entire
body.  It will only give the additional damage resistance for the body part
that it protects.  When choosing dragon armor it is important to remember
that some armor obviously gets hit more often.

Metallic Skin Resists
--------------------
Gold     - Fire, Poison
Silver   - Cold
Copper   - Acid
Bronze   - Lightning
Brass    - Charm, Fire
Steel    - Physical

Chromatic Skin Resists
--------------------
Red      - Fire
Blue     - Lightning
Green    - Poison
Black    - Acid
White    - Cold
Brown    - Fire

Gem Skin Resists
--------------------
Crystal  - Light/Harm
Topaz    - Drain
