ESSENCE
There are many types of essence that can be used in spellcrafting.  Each
is taken from a specific aspect of the world of Algoron.  Only the essence
merchants know the exact process.  Each essence imparts a specific ability
when merged with a spellcrafted gem.  The resulting gem can then be imbued
into a crafted piece of equipment.  Some essences are more powerful than
others while some have very little power at all.  Depending on the
combination of essences put into an item, the process can sometimes weaken
the item so that it requires the owner's life force to exist, if the item
does not perish altogether.

The Types of Essences are:

essence of life       HP                 essence of venom        Poison
essence of inertia    Moves              essence of confusion    Stun
essence of sight**    Hit
essence of pain**     Damage
essence of the magi*  Saves

**Gems of Pain and Sight can only be put into armor and not weapons.
*Gems of the Magi take up two slots rather than one.

      Syntax: craft <skill> <gemname> gem <essence>
      Example: craft spellcraft 'amethyst gem pain'
      Example: craft spellcraft 'diamond gem the magi

Once an essence has been placed within a spellcrafted gem, the gem is
then ready to be fused or imbued into a crafted item.  There is even
rumor told of the rare finding of the fabled ferrite upon Algoron
though its whereabouts remains unknown.

      Syntax: imbue <essence> <crafted item>
      Example: imbue pain gossamer

Only Legendary Grand Masters can imbue the more picky gems.  Specifically
the diamond gem of the magi is very fickle.  It cannot be placed next to
any other gem therefore it always takes up two slots.  The only other case
of specific gem placement occurs when two weapon essenced gems are
placed on the same weapon.  There is only a small chance of success in this
case but it can happen.  The essences inside the gems conflict with each
other and must be placed in such a manner the two gems take up four slots.
When this does occur the weapon will suffer the same weakness as stated
above and will require its owners life force to exist (Becomes Rot_Death).

Additionally, a spellcrafter can check the status of their spellcrafted
item to see the number of slots and points remaining.

      Syntax: imbue status <crafted item>
      Example: imbue status adamantium shield
      Example Line Feed: An adamantium shield has 3 slots and 16 points remaining.
