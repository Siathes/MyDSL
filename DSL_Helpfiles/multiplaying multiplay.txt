MULTIPLAYING MULTIPLAY
What is multiplay?  Multiplay can be several things but is typically when
when you have two or more of your alts online at the same time or if you
have any interaction between your alts in any capacity.  Your characters are
to remain separate of each other at ALL TIMES.  This includes-

If you have one character online while you have another character in the
creation mode or rolling for stats.

Any transfer, trade, collection or other activity where an item or money can
be passed from one of your alts to another one of your alts, not limited to
the direct hand to hand pass, dropping the items or money in a location with
one alt only to be picked up by another alt of yours or using a third party
(or fourth, etc) as a go between to "give" the item or money to another one
of your alts.

Any passing of information that you learn with one character and use on
another one character of yours for either RP or PK purposes.  You cannot
write notes to your other alts or forward notes along to your other alts.
This also includes scouting with one character (clanned or not) or
asking/learning about PKP that is currently ongoing and relogging another
alt to attempt/secure a kill.

Multiplaying is illegal and tracked and is a serious offense in the game.
If you are found to be multiplaying in any way, YOU, ANY ALTS INVOLVED AND
ANY OTHER PLAYER FOUND TO PARTICIPATE IN THE MULTIPLAYING WILL BE DENIED
WITHOUT WARNING.  If you wish to appeal your penalty, please send an email
of explanation to scorn@dsl-mud.  Org.  Be sure to include the name of the
character(s) and DSL in the subject line.
