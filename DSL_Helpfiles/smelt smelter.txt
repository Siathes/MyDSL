SMELT SMELTER

This is a 2nd tier craft that requires the smelter to get their ore from
another player after it has been mined.  Pricing in acquiring the ore can
vary as this is set by the individual crafters. Smelting requires you be
in a room with a smelting furnace.  These are quite expensive and likely
only found in kingdom halls or clan halls.  You will also require the
correct type of ore to smelt the metal you wish to make.

     Syntax: smelt <metal type>

               Metal types       Ore types

               bronze bar        (copper)
               iron bar          (iron)
               steel bar         (coal)
               alloy bar         (nickel)
               fine alloy bar    (titanium)
               mithril bar       (mithril)
               adamantium bar    (adamant)
               asterite bar      (asterite)
               netherium bar     (netherium)
               arcanium bar      (arcanium)

Note that if you have an alt that mines and smelts, you cannot use material
gained from one in order to benefit the other.  See multiplaying rules.
