SUMMON FELBEAST

Syntax: cast 'summon felbeast'

LORE:

Early explorers from Storm Keep and Verminasia who dared venture into the
far reaches of the Rip returned with tales of strange denizens and creatures
within the otherworldly plane including an elusive feline predator.  Named
after their bony horns, scaled hide, and scything claws, the felbeast has
itself proven a territorial, foul-tempered guardian said to offer its
respect and obedience only to those graced by Drakkara.  Disciples of the
Sanctum on their trials crossing beyond the Rip into the Infinite Night
found the Felbeast was drawn to wielders of the Umbra.  Moreover, should a
bond be forged with such a creature, it may be called upon and serve as a
mount for servants of the Dark Queen.
