GEMS

There are eight types of spellcrafting gemstones and each will each hold a
certain number of spellcraft points.  As a spellcrafter improves thier skill
they gain access to more powerful gems.  Diamond gems are always capable of
holding eight spellcraft points.  In addition, depending on the level of
the crafted item that gems are being fused into, there are a certain amount
of slots that can be used to place gems in to create a variety of effects.

                                            Standard Equipment Available
  Gem Type    Points Available                 Level   Slots   Points
                                     |
  Obsidian       1 Point             |          1-9      1       2
  Moonstone      2 Points            |         10-19     1       4
  Opal           3 Points            |         20-24     2       6
  Amethyst       4 Points            |         25-29     2       8
  Emerald        5 Points            |         30-34     2       12
  Sapphire       6 Points            |         35-39     3       16
  Ruby           7 Points            |         40-44     4       20
  Diamond        8 Points            |         45-49     5       24
                                               50-51     6       28

Example- For a spellcrafter to create a level 51 helmet with +3 dam,
    +25 mana and -1 saves, this will take up all 28 points - 12 points
    for the damage (overcharged diamond), 8 for the mana (regular diamond)
    and 8 for the saves (regular diamond).  However, only 4 slots would be
    used of the 6 slots available as the diamond of the magi uses 2 slots.
