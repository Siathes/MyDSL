GATE

Syntax: cast gate <target>

The gate spell is a powerful transportation magic that opens up a portal
between your character and another person or creature somewhere else in the
world.  This portal will transport you and any pet you might have, but not
other members of your group.

Players and creatures more than three (3) levels higher than yours, cannot
be gated to.  All.  God rooms, private rooms, and no recall rooms cannot be
gated to, and Rooms flagged as imm, private rooms or no recall cannot be
gated to, and Imms as well as players with no_summon set, are immune to
gate.

NOTE: Clan members may not be gated to except by their fellow clan members.
Gating to a kingdomer is considered kingdom/clan interference and is
illegal.

Gate is not powerful enough to cross vast oceans to other continents.
