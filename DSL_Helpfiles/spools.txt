        Notespools

    Like channels, there are various notespools that serve different
      purposes. This will be a short overview of each notespool's
      function. If you want to learn how to compose notes, type help
      note. Again, read help rules before composing notes.
    Note - This is the IC notespool for generic notes. Anything from
      wedding invitations to death warrants go here.
    ANote - The auction note spool. This is for people who're selling or
      looking for extremely rare items.
    OOCN - This is the Out of Character notespool to post just about
      anything that you want to share with fellow players.
    QNote - The quest note spool is for topics relating to quests. This
      is where future quests are announced or the results of past quests
      are posted.
    StoryN - Story notes are written by players about their characters.
      These notes are usually OOC narrations of IC events, so don't use
      knowledge gained from reading these notes in role-play.
    History - History notes are accounts posted by the immortal staff of
      large events. Take some time to read over a few of these to get an
      understanding of Algoron's history and politics.
    News - News notes are posted by the immortal staff to inform you of
      upcoming events, game updates, server downtime, rules, etc.
    Changes - Change notes are posted by the immortal staff to inform you
      of gameplay changes.

    List notes by typing (note type) list. List unread notes by
      typing (note type) ulist. You can read notes by entering
      (note type) read #. So,type storyn ulist to get a list of
      unread story notes, then storyn read 125 to read the story note
      labelled as number 125.

        Type HELP NEWBIE to return to the table of contents.
