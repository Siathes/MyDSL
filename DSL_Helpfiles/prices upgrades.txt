HOMES AND LAIRS -

Private homes are a 4 room maximum built within a kingdom's city limits.
They must be constructed within a kingdom's housing district and will need
the Kingdom Leader's permission to build.

Lairs are for remorts only and can be built most anywhere outside of a
kingdom's city limits.  Lairs are a 2 room minimum, up to 4 rooms maximum.
Limit of 2 warders allowed.

Home and lair boosts max out at 200%.  See Private Home for additional
options and pricing. See Help Housing for building criteria, do's and dont's.

House in the city .................. 15 Eggs per room - New or Remodel
House outside the city ............ 100 Eggs per room - Remort Only
Lair Room Remodel .................. 25 Eggs per room - Remodel Only - Location change is full cost
Nighttime Description .............. 10 Eggs per room house - New or Remodel
Nighttime Description .............. 20 Eggs per room lair - New or Remodel
Regen Rate Increase ................ 20 Eggs per 10% boost hp or mana

PERSONAL TOWERS -

Personal Towers are available only through cards and are single owner.
Unlike houses and lairs where you can add additional players to have
uninvited access, these will require each owner to invite in others.  Like
housing, remorts including giants cannot be owners of towers nor should
remorts find sanctuary within any tower.  Towers follow all lair pricing for
any additions and a helpfile will be coming soon.
