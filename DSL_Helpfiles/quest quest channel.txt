QUEST, QUEST CHANNEL
Syntax:  quest <message>
Like other public channels, quest sends a message to all characters in
the world who are listening to the channel.  This channel is used for
running or participating in quests.  Anyone may run a quest, though it
is most commonly done by immortals.  Quests often, but not always
result in prizes for the successful completion of a quest.  Quests may
be roleplaying in nature, in which case, all conversation on the
channel should attempt to remain in character.  Trivia quests and
occasional other quests will be out of character and at these times
either in character or out of character conversation related to the
quest is welcome on this channel.
See Also:  PQUEST
