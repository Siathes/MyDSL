kdeposit
Syntax:  kdeposit <number>
This command may be used at a bank to deposit a number of jeweled eggs
directly from your inventory into your kingdom's bank account.  You may
only deposit jeweled eggs, and they must be directly in your inventory,
not in a container.
See also: 'BANK' 'MONEY' 'WITHDRAW' 'DEPOSIT' 'CDEPOSIT'
