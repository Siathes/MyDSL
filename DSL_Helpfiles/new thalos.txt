NEW THALOS

New Thalos, the Jewel of the Desert, is a neutral kingdom that lies on the
Ishtar River near the coast and is a thriving port city.  It is the only
kingdom that has managed to thrive in the desert lands and remains an oasis
for the weary traveller.

No one race predominates New Thalos and representatives from nearly all of
Algoran's races can be found here.  All work together with the common goal
of ultimately making New Thalos the strongest Kingdom on Algoron.  New
Thalos is a monarchy based government ruled by a powerful Sultanate who
takes an active role in leadership and development of New Thalos.
