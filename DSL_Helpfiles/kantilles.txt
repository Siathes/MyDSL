KANTILLES

Born of Taliena and Austinian, Kantilles is the devoted and unwavering
protector of magic.  Depicted as a young man with breath-taking looks,
Kantilles often walks the realm sharing his knowledge of the magics with
those he deems worthy.

Kantilles has often been seen performing cantrips for small children and
even setting up magic shows for whole towns.  Kantilles is also known for
his wonderful sense of humor, taking delight in the good things mortals do.

He has also been known to be a fierce competitor and a champion of goodness.
His followers tend to be magic-users, but are by no means limited to them.
Kantilles takes all who respect magic under his tutlege, as he oftentimes
instructs them on how to better their magical talents.
