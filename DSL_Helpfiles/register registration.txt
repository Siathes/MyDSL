REGISTER REGISTRATION
The Register command works during the creation process by allowing you to
choose a guild (class).  When you first create a character, you may notice
that your guild is set to citizen.  By entering the proper guildmaster's
room and typing 'register', you will join one of the four basic guilds
(warrior, mage, cleric, thief).  The Register command also works during play
by allowing a non-grouped* character the ability to change his/her hometown.
By going to the registration mob in the desired hometown and typing
'register', this only changes the players recall town. This does not allow
access to the kingdom rooms, notes and other ammenities. If you are in a
kingdom, useing this command will strip you of all kingdom flags, even if
you register in the same kingdom.
*Non-grouped refers to any character who is not a part of the clan
system, the Austinians, or the Marauders.
