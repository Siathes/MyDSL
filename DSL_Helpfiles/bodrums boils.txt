BODRUMS BOILS

Named for (one of) the most infamous of all of Drakkara's Black Robes,
Bodrums Boils is an insidious curse devised in the heart of the Conclave's
least ethical laboratories.  When used upon a victim already suffering under
poison, the boils will curdle the victim's veins, reducing their ability to
resist further maladiction and assault.

This power is fickle, however, and necromancers who do not operate under the
auspices of the Conclave's permissions may find the magic refusing to obey
them.
