COMBINE
Syntax:  combine
This command is used to toggle between different forms of displaying inventory.
The primary effect is to display multiple items of the same name on separate
lines or to combine them, using a number to indicate how many of that item you
have.  As a secondary effect, the "combined" form of listing inventory will
list each item indented from the left margin and the "long form" will show each
item beginning at the left margin.
See also:  INVENTORY
