PRESTIGE

Characters that have played in a clan or kingdom for at least 1000 hours,
can be eligible for prestige within their respective circles.  The hours
that a player serves in their kingdom or clan are the minimal requirements
that are needed for recommendation.

In addition to the hours, a player should have significant and established
Rp within their kingdom/clan, as well as established roleplay outside of
their kingdom/clan that has been noteworthy in many areas, globally or with
historical impact.

This is a roleplay honor and will require demonstrative and meaningful
interactions, over a length of time.  It could be possible that a player
could have over 10k hours on a character before a quest is presented to a
player.

Players will not be told of the pass/fail quest which will be Rp immersive
and designed to challenge your character.

Should you pass, you will get a passive 5% bonus to your damage output and a
(*).

The (*) represents characters who are famous or infamous.  These are people
that when someone in the world thinks of a clan, kingdom, or group, these
are the characters that have had a sustained and long term influence and
immediately come to mind.

Should you decide to leave your kingdom or clan, the restoration of your (*)
would need Admin approval PRIOR to your departure, along with validated RP
on your departure and your (*)'s return.

Prestige Hours:

For players that have accumulated over 1k in prestige hours and wish to
retrain, they can request that these hours be restored to them, upon
retraining and returning to the same kingdom/clan they were in, at the time
of the request.

This request must be made to Admin via note, to approve and once approved,
the player will need to pray for a Senior Imm/Admin (level 3 or higher) to
verify their prestige hours.  You will then have 24 hours to retrain,
reguild and request prestige hours be restored, via pray for a Senior
Imm/Admin.
