KADIYA

Kadiya tends to share the view of her mother regarding bloodshed.  Born of
Taliena and Austinian, Kadiya has always been somewhat of a conflict stopper
since she was a child.

Kadiya has been known to stand over innocent victims of savagery and weep
endlessly, even going so far as to bury the corpse.  She always attempts to
contact empires before wars can begin, sharing with them the benefits that
peacetime bring.

Her followers tend to disregard battle and are only asked to use it as a
means of self-defense, or for the general vanquishing of evil.

When she appears she does so with little, if any notice to the mortals.  She
has mostly been seen at the sight of great battles, weeping for the lost
ones.
