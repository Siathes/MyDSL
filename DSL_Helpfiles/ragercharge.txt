RAGERCHARGE

Syntax: ragercharge

Rager charge is a skill unique to battleragers.  Upon being completely
enraged, the rager charges through a room swinging his/her axes at anything
and everything in sight (and/or out of sight).

When in such a rage, the battlerager takes no heed of friends, foes or
otherwise.  The battlerager's movements are so erratic that even those
hiding and sneaking sometimes have to dodge out of the way of the swinging
axes.
