SHIP PORT PRICES
These prices are only for organizations.  Individuals can not purchase
ships and ports.
Private Dock . . . . . . . . . . . . . 100 eggs
(Includes two-way portal to and
from clan hall/kingdom hall.
Can only be accessed via the portal.)
Water Way Rights and Port Taxes . . . . 1 egg / month
(Only clans have to pay this. These
fees go to the kingdom in which the
port is located.)

Ship Upgrades:

Larger Sails. . . . . . . . . . . . . . 20 eggs per 3 yards of cloth

purchase any ships.
