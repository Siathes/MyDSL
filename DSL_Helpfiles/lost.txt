lost
If you lost your password to a character, there is only ONE way to get it
back.

First, you MUST have set your email address in the oocset command while
logged in as that player.

Second, you must email Scorn from that email address requesting the password
be cut and paste from another character as yours.

If your email addresses do not match, you will most likely not get your
password fixed.

You can email Scorn at scorn@dsl-mud.org.
