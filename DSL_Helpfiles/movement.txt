        Navigation and Status

    To move your character, type the direction you wish to move. A
    list of exits is displayed in the room description. The possible
    directions are up, down, north, south, west, east, northeast,
    southeast, northwest, and southwest. Some MUD clients allow you
    to use the number pad to move easily.

    To open a door, type open (direction).
    To see the room description again, type look.
    To examine an object, type look (object) or examine (object).
    To see who is currently in the same area as you, type where.
    To see all visible players online, type who.
    To return to your recall point, type recall.
    To see detailed information about your character, type score.
    To see spell affects actively effecting your character,
      type affects.
    If a character becomes hungry or thirsty, you can eat (food)
      or drink (container). If you don't eat or drink for a while,
      your character can become fatigued, in which you need to eat and
      drink, then rest.
    To fill a drink container, type fill (container) in a room
      with a fountain.
    You can empty the container by typing empty (container)
    If you are injured or are low on mana, type rest or sleep.
      Resting has better regeneration than standing, but prohibits you
      from moving. Sleeping has better regeneration than resting, but
      prohibits you from doing most commands.
    (in Althainia --)
    The water fountain is south of recall.
    A vendor that sells barrels is south and east twice from recall.
    The baker is south twice, west, and north from recall.
