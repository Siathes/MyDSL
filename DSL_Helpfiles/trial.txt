TRIAL
Any mortal who feels like they have been treated unfairly in a penalty
situation may request a review from the Implmentor.  You should write Scorn
a note and he will get back to you ASAP to get your story.  He will then
contact the immortal to get his story and will decide whether or not your
were fairly treated.  However, be aware that the decesion can be made that
your penalty was indeed to light, in which you will get an increased
penalty.  You may also contact Kyri to reivew penalties.
