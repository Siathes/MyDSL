ENTER
Syntax:  enter portal
         enter <portal name>
         enter n.portal
Enter is a means of entering magical portals between rooms.  These
may be portals which are permanently part of an area or portals which
have been created with the transportation spell group.
If there is only one portal in the room, or you wish to enter the
first portal in a room with more than one, the syntax enter portal
can be used.  If there are two or more portals in a room, the portal
name or a number should be used. Look in portal, may now be used to see
the destination of the portal.
