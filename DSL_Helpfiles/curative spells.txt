'CURATIVE SPELLS'

The curative spells are used to heal various unpleasant conditions that can
befall an adventurer.   For healing of damage, see the healing spell group.
Curative spells cannot be used by mages or thieves.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help (spellname).
The curative spell group consists of the following spells:

cure blindness  restores sight to the blind
cure disease    heals the plague
cure poison     removes the harmful effects of poison
cure fatigue    restores vitality to the affected individual
cure bugbite    removes the toxic effects of the bugbear bite

This group is available to the following classes -
    BATTLERAGER CLERIC CRUSADER DRAGON DRUID GIANT
    PALADIN PRIEST SHADOWKNIGHT RANGER SHAMAN WARRIOR
