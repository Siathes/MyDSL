TURPA

Born of Kwainin and Zandreya, Turpa learned to control the weather with the
help of her mother and father.  She is so adept in her power of the weather
that her attitude reflects the weather of the world.  When it is stormy, she
is said to be unapproachable, while on soft spring days, when the sun is
warm, she is as kind as can be to those who follow her.

Rumor has it that when it is stormy, and she bathes in the sea, the storm
abates and flees from her into a calm serenity that she prefers.

She, when she lets herself be seen by mortals, appears in a long grey dress.
She is reported to have long, flowing black hair and albino eyes, the
perfect balance between darkness and light.  Her beauty is that of her
mother, with unmarred flesh and delicate features, she is most definitely a
force to be reckoned with.
