DOUBLES DONATION

Syntax: donate <donation type> <eggs>

 donate double-exp 10
 donate - without any other syntax will show the donation board

This command will allow any player(s) to control when the double experience
boost is turned on, for a cost.  There is no multiplay concern with using
this command between alts, just don't be blatant with it please :)

The cost to turn on double experience is 25 eggs, for a 24 hour period, for
a global impact.  One player or multiple players can contribute to the
donation offering provided there are eggs in their bank account to
contribute.  Once your funding contributions (individual or collective)
reaches a total of 25 eggs, the double experience boost will turn on and
remain on for all players, for a 24 hour period.

Every increment of 25 eggs will either turn on the experience boost for 24
hours or extend the boost for an additional 24 hours.

Any player can offer a 'donation' at any point in time, even if the boost is
already active.  Once 25 eggs has been accumulated and the boost is
activated, an automatic thank you OOCN will generate to all, noting the
player(s) that made this possible!

Examples:

Player 1 donates 3 eggs, Player 2 donates 15 eggs, Player 3 donates 20 eggs -

Double experience will turn on for 24 hours with 13 eggs remaining in the
'donation pot' that will go toward the next 24 hour boost, once it reaches
25 eggs total.

Player 1 donates 75 eggs -

Double experience will turn on for 72 hours.
