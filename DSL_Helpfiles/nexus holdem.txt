NEXUS HOLDEM
Nexus Holdem is a poker game where each player gambles for the best five
card hand out of seven total cards.  Before cards are dealt the blinds must
be paid.  This ensures there is always money to gamble for.  They are called
blinds because you have to pay them before you see your cards.  The player
to the left of the dealer pays the small blind.  The player to the left of
the small blind pays the big blind.  Once the blinds are paid each player
gets to cards face down.  Then each person gets the option to call or raise
the big blind or fold.  Once that round of betting has finished three cards
are dealt face up in the middle of the table.  These are community cards,
everyone gets to use them along with thier two hole cards to make a hand.
Those three cards are called the flop.  After the flop there is a betting
round.  Then another community card is dealt face up.  This is the turn
card.  After the turn card there is a round of betting.  Then the final
community card is dealt face up.  This is the river card.  After the river
card there is a round of betting.  Then everyone left shows thier hand.  The
best hand wins the pot.  Only five cards can be used so if two or more hands
are identical the pot is split between them.
