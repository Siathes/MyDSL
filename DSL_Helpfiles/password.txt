PASSWORD
{YPASSWORD{x

{cSyntax:{x password <old password> <new password>

Password is the command to change your character's password.  The first
argument must be your old password.  The second argument is what you want
your new password to be.  Password changes are protected and not seen by
Imms.

{yFor Lost Passwords:{x

{cEmail:{x {WScorn@dsl-mud.org{x or {WCayenna.dsl@gmail.com{x

If you lose your password, you need to email either Scorn or Cayenna with a
request to change your password to match that of another character you have.
If the email field in your pfile does NOT match that of the email you send
to Scorn or Cayenna, your email will be ignored.

To change your player files email address, use: oocset email

{rNOTE:{x Cayenna's process is slightly different, please don't share your
password in the email, you will be assigned a new one that you will be asked
to change.
