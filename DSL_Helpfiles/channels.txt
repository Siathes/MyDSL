CHANNELS

Syntax: Channels

This command results in a listing of all the channels of
communication and the status of each (i.e. whether they are
turned on or off).  The channels for public communication on DSL include:

OOC - ooc (for Out Of Character discussions)
Newbie - ooc (for newbie related questions)
Ask/Answer - ooc (for game related questions)
BB - ooc (any out of character discussions relating to the bloodbath)
Oking - ooc (for out of character discussions in the kingdom)
Oclan - ooc (for out of character discussions in the clan)
Grats - ooc (to cheer on and congratulate others for levelling)
Radio - ooc (for radio program related discussions)
Kingdom - In Character (for kingdom only related discussions)
Clan - In Character (for clan only related discussions)
Gossip - In Character (strictly for IN CHARACTER discussions)
Auction - In Character, the only allowable ooc is stats and item level
Cgos - In Character discussions concerning clan or pkill matters
Thaxanos - In Character (for discussions between the clan and kingdom)
Shalonesti - In Character (for discussions between the clan and kingdom)
Quest - Dependent on what is being run at the time, whether it is IC or OOC

Spamming, or repeating things multiple times, on a public channel,
using foul offensive or abusive language, making threats,
or otherwise abusing channels may result in your use of them being revoked.

* You may use color to advertise your shop and shop only on auction channel
Be tasteful and not color obtrusive (ie using every color you can)
If you abuse this, you will be penalized and you may not make it appear
like another channel that exists.
