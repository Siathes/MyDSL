PQUEST QUESTTYPE

syntax:  pquest request find
         pquest request hunt
         pquest info
         pquest complete
         pquest clear

         questtype points
         questtype experience

There are certain mobs in Algoron who are able to assign and oversee
personal quests for characters.  When in the presence of these mobs, you can
request a personal quest using the syntax shown.  You may request two
different types of quests.  Pquest request find will send you to find an
item.  Pquest request hunt will send you to hunt and kill a beast.

While on a quest, you may use the "info" syntax to get a reminder of what
the goal of your quest is.

When you have completed the assigned quest, you must return to the mob that
assigned the quest and use the "complete" syntax to obtain your reward.

If you feel that you are unable to complete a quest in the time allocated or
feel that it is beyond your ability to complete use the "clear" syntax to
clear the quest.  Of course, the questor will not be to pleased with you.

Personal quest rewards come in the form of gold and quest points.  Quest
points are shown in your score and may be saved up and used to purchase
special items from the quest mob.

There is a time delay between quests, and the quest mob resets when the game
does so that until the quest mob code is changed most characters will have
time delays following crashes and reboots.

You may also select whether you wish to quest for a quest points reward, or
an exprerience reward.  At the questmaster, select a questtype of experience
or points.
