        {RApproaching Recruiters{x

    An important part of your character's life will be getting into a clan
      or kingdom. The recruiting process is completely in-character. Don't
      say anything Out of Character. It is required that you have a
      description before you can be recruited, so check the RP section on
      the website for assistance. Again, the URL is
      {Bhttp://www.dsl-mud.org/rp.{x

    First, figure out which kingdom or clan you want to join. Read through
      their individual help files to help make your decision. Once you've
      done so, type {cwhok (kingdom) recruiter{x or {cwhoc (clan) recruiter{x.
      So, if you were looking to join New Thalos, you would type {cwhok
      New_Thalos recruiter{x. If you wanted to join Shadow, you would type
      {cwhoc Shadow recruiter{x. Send the recruiter a tell once you're
      prepared for a recruitment session.

    If they approve of you, they will ask you to invite them. Simply
      {cinvite (name){x and they will guild you.

    On a final note, beware of joining clans unless you're confident you
      know the game well enough. Many clanners have been playing for years
      and know every trick and tactic to kill you. Clanning is only for
      people who've played the game long enough to know the areas and
      mechanics of the game and are looking for a new challenge.
