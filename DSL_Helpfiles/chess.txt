chess
"Chess" Is the command used to do ALL chess game commands, as well as
watching.

The game will NOT declare check mate.  This is INTENTIONAL.  It is up to the
players to decide if a mate has occured and the losing person must type
'chess resign' to end the game (since they will not be able to make any
legal moves.)

Chess watch allows someone to watch a chess game in progress.  They must be
in the same room and cannot be playing a game their self.

Chess start starts a game with someone else.

Chess rejoin allows you to restart a game after quitting out, going LD,
reboot, etc.

Chess kcastle will allow you to castle king side

Chess qcastle will allow you to castle queen side

Chess history will show your movement history.

Chess show will show the current boards lay out as well as taken, and took,
pieces.

Chess promote will allow you to promote a pawn to a ROOK, QUEEN, KNIGHt or
BISHOP.
