MUDSCHOOL

The mudschool is designed to help new players familiarize themselves with
DSL. Inside is an academy designed to teach DSL's commands and RP, an
arena full of creatures to fight and gain experience, a universal trainer,
a pet shop for those players that find themselves having difficulty
fighting on their own and a store that sells basic supplies for new
characters.

The mudschool can be reached by going up from most bindstones. You can
leave the mudschool at any time by typing 'recall'.  This will bring you
to the temple within your home kingdom.  However, once you reach level 6,
you will no longer be able to move inside the mudschool and will be forced
to recall.  Please note that not all kingdoms have an easily accessible
mudschool near them.
