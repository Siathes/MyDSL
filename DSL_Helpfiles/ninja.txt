NINJA
{YNINJA{x

Ninja are experts of covert warfare hailing from the exotic continent of
Shokono.  They are skilled in the art of stealth attacks and infiltration,
being particularly adept at blending into their surroundings.  As such, they
prefer not to don anything more bulky than leather armor as to ensure that
their speed is maintained in order to deliver strikes that are deadly and
accurate.  This also allows for the ninja to be able to slip away just as
quickly.
