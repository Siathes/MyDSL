WATCHER WATCHERS

Each clan and kingdom has an Immortal Representative known as a watcher.  A
watcher's purpose is to be there if clans or kingdoms wish to ask for
advice, give explanations, help ensure that requests for the clan and
kingdom are being attended to, and even to run quests.

The purpose of a watcher is to be a point of contact between the players and
the immortals, to monitor activity in that location, check in with
leadership to see if there are needs, challenges, ideas, etc. that should be
bubbled up to Admin.  Watchers should also facilitating small bits of Rp
for their areas as well as engage in preliminary conversation around
possible leadership changes and getting information to Admin for final
approval.

Kingdoms and Clans can also request another watcher through Admin.
