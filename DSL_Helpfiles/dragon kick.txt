DRAGON KICK

Syntax: dragon kick <target>

An especially powerful kicking skill, available only to dragons.  The
command may be used without a target if combat is already in progress.

NOTE: It is illegal for dragons to kick guards out of cities to avoid being
attacked as this circumvents game code.
