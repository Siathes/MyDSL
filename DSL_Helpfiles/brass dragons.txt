'BRASS DRAGONS'
Like blue dragons, brasses favor arid, sandy regions where the hot sun can
properly toast their shiny scales. Although good-natured, these dragons
are selfish, boisterous, and overly proud. Their weakness is their desire
for small talk. And because they know dozens of languages, it is easy for
them to find other creatures with whom to converse. Despite their love for
gab, brass dragons have little interest in art or culture. They are
benevolent and understanding, but they ultimately believe the weak should
be weeded from the strong, and superior beings allowed to flourish. Brass
dragons prefer to attack with their claws and teeth, saving their magic
for dire situations. They can choose between breathing a cloud of sleeping
gas or a wave of heat.
