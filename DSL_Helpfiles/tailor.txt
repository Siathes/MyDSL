TAILOR

This third tier craft differs from weapons and armorcrafting as there are
times that a crafter can work independantly and at other times, will need
the refined materials from a tanner.  Tailors can purchase threads and
cloth squares from a vendor found within most kingdoms.  The two alone can
be sewn together to create various doll trinkets.  Though tailors also work
with leather and will need to mix in various types of leather squares when
they are creating their saddle leather trinkets in order to master their
craft.  The more advanced you become, the better your skill in working with
the materials which will result in fewer failed attempts and ruined
supplies.

      Syntax: craft <skill> <item>

      Example: craft tailor 'woolen doll trinket'
      Example: craft tailor 'deer leather saddle trinket'
