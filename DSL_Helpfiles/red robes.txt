'RED ROBES'
[ Red Robes ]

The Red Robes represent the balance and neutral magics of the land.  They
draw their magical ability from the Red Moon.  Those of the Red Tower seek
to better understand the workings of the world, both good and evil.
Maintaining the balance between these great powers is their eternal task.

As mages, they also seek to further the Art, the power of magic, throughout
the realm.  The magic is their life, it is the binding force between them
and the other towers of the Conclave.
