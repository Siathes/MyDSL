SICCARA

Siccara, the Goddess of Healing, is born of Austinian and Taliena.  Her
talents in the healing magicks are unparalleled, even among her immortal
peers.

In the tome of Legends, it was reported by prophets that Siccara was
evidently the victim of Devion's fancy.  Devion attempted to lure Siccara
into his world, but was thwarted by Nadrik and Siccara working together to
push Devion back.

Siccara has been known to visit the lands and share her talents with
everything pure of heart.  She has been seen tending to wounded trees and
land, along with her gentle caring of mortals wounded in battle.  Her
followers tend to share their healing powers to aid those in need and pure
of heart.
