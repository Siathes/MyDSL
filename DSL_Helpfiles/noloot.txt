NOLOOT
Syntax:  noloot
This command toggles whether or not you will allow other characters to loot
items from your corpse.  It can be helpful to allow your corpse to be looted
if someone is helping to retrieve your gear, but if you are able to get back
to your corpse yourself, it's best not to allow others to take things from
your corpse so you can retrieve your own equipment without fear of it being
stolen by the time you get back to your corpse.
See also:  AUTO
