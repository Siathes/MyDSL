POSE CHECKPOSE
Syntax: pose <string without ending punctuation>
Syntax: checkpose

The pose command is used to set your characters pose while you are not
moving.  You would use this to show what your character looks like when
others look in the room, or enter the room.  The description will still have
the standard "is here", or "sleeping", etc, you can simply add additional
info

For example, you could be standing in a room and type: "pose reading a
book".  To everyone in the room, this would look like: Person is here,
reading a book.

Typing pose by itself will clear your current pose.

Do not abuse this for pk purposes.  If you move, your pose will clear, and
if you change position your pose will clear.

Typing checkpose will let you see what you look like in the room from others
point of view, this can be used at any time.

EMOTE PMOTE
Syntax: emote <action>
Syntax: pmote <action>

EMOTE is used to express emotions or actions. Besides EMOTE, there are several dozen
built-in social commands, such as CACKLE, HUG and THANK

PMOTE is similar to emote, but will substitute you and your for a target's
name (i.e. pmote bonks Scorn will be read as bonks you by Scorn, and
as bonks Scorn by everyone else).
