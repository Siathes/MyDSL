dsl radio
DSL Radio is a server which allows the immortals and playerbase of DSL to
play music online as a radio station.  The DJ's can play everything from
popular music to custom made commericials for clans/humor.  Here's how to
connect to the site and begin listening.

1.) Download the full version of winamp at http://www.winamp.com
2.) Install Winamp
3.) Run Winamp
4.) hit control-L
5.) type this address in - http://radio.dsl-mud.org:8080
6.) Sit back, and either enjoy or cringe at whatever might be playing. :)
