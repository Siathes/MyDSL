SHIP RULES

You may NOT steal someone else's ship or your own kingdom/clans ship for the
hell of it and abandon it or what have you, just to be a jerk - this will
get you denied.

FIGHTING WITH SHIPS

Ship vs Ship (can have a mix of kingdom and clanner) = Legal

Ship (clanner only aboard) vs Swashbuckler and/or Dragon in the ocean = Legal

Ship (clanner only aboard) vs anyone else in the ocean = ILLEGAL

Ship (clanner, kingdomer mix) vs anything BUT another ship = Dork for all involved

Attacking another ship in their port with no one aboard that ship = ILLEGAL

Attacking another ship in their port with people aboard to defend it = Legal

Attacking another ship in another port (clanner/kingdom rules apply) - Legal

Attacking someone not on their ship in their port = Legal
(Legal because person can run away)

Attacking someone who is on land from your ship (clanner people only) = Legal
(Legal because person can run away)

Attacking a merchant ship (those ones that move on their own without people) = ILLEGAL
