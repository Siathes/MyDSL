WORSHIP
'WORSHIP SPELLS'

The worship spell group is meant only for those of clerical types.  The
spells contained within are used by those zealots that wish to display their
own views of their gods.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The worship spell group consists of the following spells:

crucify                 creates a crucifix and applies the corpse to it
holy steed              creates a steed blessed by the gods

This group is available to the following classes -
    CLERICS CRUSADERS DRAGONS PALADINS
