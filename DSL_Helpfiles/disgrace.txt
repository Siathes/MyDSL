disgrace
Disgrace Points are points given out to clans during the Clan Wars season
for various offenses.  The Clan with the most (and the least) at the end of
the season will earn prizes or lose clan regen/healer levels.  Obviously,
the one with the most will be the one losing the regen levels.

How Disgrace Points are distributed:
Clan caught cheating in Clan Wars - 50 points
Clan caught throwing a match - 35 points
Clan disqualified for breaking a Clan Wars Rule - 20 points
Clan doesn't produce any members of a bracket - 10 points
Team having less than 5 people on it - 1 Point per person missing
note: all 5 missing is 10, not 5

A clan must have at least 10 points to be eligible for the regen decrease.
A clan must have broken a 10 point rule broken to be eligible for the healer decrease.
In the event of a tie, all winners will win the prize and all losers.
