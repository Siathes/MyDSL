CLASS

A class is like a profession or career.  It is what you have trained to
become in your younger years.  Your character is assumed to have some
previous training before you start adventuring.

The character classes currently available for starting characters are:

 Warrior - The champion, swordsman, soldier and bar-room brawler.  The
warrior lives and dies by his weapon.  They can be found on the front lines
of the battle toe to toe with the enemy.  Strength and Constitution are the
warrior's main attributes.

For more help see: help warrior

 Thief - Scouts, pick-pockets, and all around sneaks.  The thief lives by
his quick wits and feet.  They can be found behind the enemy stabbing him in
the back, or doing other sneaky things outside of combat.  Dexterity is the
thief's main attribute.

For more help see: help thief

 Cleric - The protector and the healer.  The clerist lives by his faith to a
god who grants the cleric magical spells.  While mostly defensive, the
cleric can stand his ground on any battle field.  Wisdom is the clerist's
main attribute.

For more help see: help cleric

 Mage - The master of magical energies.  The mage's power comes from arcane
texts and years of study.  The mage, while weak in hand to hand combat, is
able to call upon powerful magic to destroy or charm his foes.  Intelligence
is the main attribute for mages.

For more help see: help mage

 Bard - Entertainers, singers, poets, and known bar room drunks.  Bards are
a class of musicians that know some of the fighting skills, mostly the ones
they would pick up in bar fights, and then they have their repertoire of
songs which produce varying affects.

For more help see: help bard

After adventuring for a while, a character may specialize or broaden the
scope of their skills.

For more information on this option see: help reclass.
