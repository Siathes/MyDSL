'ELEMENTAL SPELLS'

The elemental spells allow the caster to control various aspects of the
elements around them.  This spell group is limited though, to those classes
of a more clerical nature.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The elemental spell group consists of the following spells:

blizzard            creates a blizzard, doing cold damage to everyone present
firestorm           creates a firestorm, doing heat damage to everyone present
summon elemental    summons an element of one of four forms to aid the caster

This group is available to the following classes -
    CLERIC DRUID PRIEST
