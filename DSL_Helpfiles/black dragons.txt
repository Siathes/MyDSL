'BLACK DRAGONS'
These great, slimy lizards usually make their homes in marshes and
jungles. Dark as the bogs they dwell in, black dragons are difficult to
notice. Black dragons are impulsive and nervous, tending to act first and
think later. Black dragons are unpredictable in what they hold precious.
Some dragons have been known to amass and covet piles of coins and gems,
while great wyrms have collected only weapons and possessions of men,
which they themselves cannot use. Black dragons breathe a deadly stream of
acid, and are quick to fight with their claws, wings, tail, and vicious
teeth.
