'BRONZE DRAGONS'
Bronze dragons lair near fresh or salt water, and near settlements of
humans and demi-humans. They are fond of treasure, but only seek it from
evil resources. Bronze dragons dislike killing, though they are fascinated
by war, and enjoy defeating evil creatures. They are very curious about
humans and demi-humans. They prefer attacking creatures smaller than
themselves with tail, teeth, and claws, reserving spells for intelligent
opponents.  Bronze dragons can breathe a cloud of fear gas that repulses
those it touches, and a bolt of lightning.
