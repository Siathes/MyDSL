DRAGONLANCE

Syntax: dragonlance

A dragon slayer knows best the type of weapon needed for fighting dragons
and is able to transform or enhance a polearm into a dragonlance.  This
destroys the existing polearm and creates a new one.  Do not use this skill
if you are wielding a polearm you care about.


SEE ALSO:  DRAGON SLAYER, DRAGON STRIKE, JAWBIND, RECLASS, SLAYER,
           SUMMON SLAYER GRYFFON, SUPERVOUCHER
