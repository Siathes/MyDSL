FINISHER FSET

Finishers are your characters finishing moves when defeating someone or
something in combat.  The default messages are random, but you do have the
ability to set your own custom message.

Cost is 1 blue diamond.  Pray for an immortal when you have it ready.

Note:  These messages are seen by all in the room.
Note:  Do not use words like 'you' or 'yours' in the message.
Note:  No excessive color and messages must be appropriate.

Please have your message ready before praying for a fset.

Code Key:

See help restring for message key codes.
