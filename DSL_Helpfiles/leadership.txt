LEADERSHIP - KINGDOM or CLAN

Leadership is not an easy thing for anyone to have to do.  They are there to
help guide and develop the faction that they are leading, but they are not
your personal entertainment tool.  How successful a kingdom or a clan is, is
dependent on the people that are in it, not just the leadership.  So keep in
mind that they are there to enjoy the game as much as you are.  That being
said, there are rules on HOW to go about changing leadership.

1.  IF you want to step down for your leadership postion...  You contact
your watcher and admin.

2.  IF you want to mutiny the leader..  Read help mutiny / help kmutiny,
contact your watcher and admin.

3.  IF you want to pass off leadership...  Contact your watcher and admin.

ALWAYS contact your watcher and admin first BEFORE acting upon changing
leadership.  There is a process we must go through before we agree to
allowing someone to lead.  There are things you may not know, that we do,
that does not make them good leadership material.

IF you are not happy with your current leadership, then speak to your
watcher and admin about it.  Please do not come to us saying 'Do something
about our absent leadership'.  You can always see about mutinying them, but
as was stated previously, it requires people willing to do stuff to make a
place great.  If you are not willing to step up to help the problem, then
how do you expect us to help you improve it?
