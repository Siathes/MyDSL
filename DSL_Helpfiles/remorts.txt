REMORTS

Dragons are awarded to people by the Implementors for either outstanding and
persistant roleplaying, or to winners of major quests.  (and once in a blue
moon, a minor quest) Asking for a Dragon auto-eliminates you from being
chosen.

Angels, Demons, and Balanx must be applied for.  They are going to be a
limited number of these beings in the game so don't get your hopes up.
Angels, Demons, and Balanx need to be applied for by either emailing or
noting Scorn.

Giants are a lesser form of remort that have a greater availability in the
realm.
