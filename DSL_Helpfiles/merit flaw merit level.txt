MERIT FLAW 'MERIT LEVEL'

Characters can elect to receive a merit or a flaw at the end of their
creation period.  Choosing a merit or flaw will add or deduct creation
points from your character at the time of creation.

If you choose a specific merit, it will add 10 creation points to your
character.  If you choose to take a random merit, it will add 8 creation
points to your character.  If you choose to take a specific flaw, it will
subtract 3 creation points from your character.  If you choose to take a
random flaw, it will subtract 5 creation points from your character.

PLEASE NOTE - Certain flaws have a DRASTIC effect on your character's
abilities.  An example is the mute flaw.  If you are mute, you CAN'T talk,
which means you can't cast spells, send tells, and potential other things.

Characters that have not chosen a merit or flaw at creation can choose to
'quest' for a merit level once they have completed their training.  You can
only quest for a merit.  You cannot quest for a flaw.  This is called
questing for a 'merit level'.

To begin work on a merit level, go to your guildmaster and type 'merit'.
You will now have a notification in your score that you have 30000
experience points to achieve before you receive your merit (understand, the
merit is completely random).

You will find that you get much less experience per kill for a merit level
than for a regular level.  If you reclass, you will not be able to continue
gathering experience for a merit level until you have again reached level
51.  Also, if you die while working toward your merit level, you will lose
ALL experience gained toward that merit level.
