SCRIPTING TRIGGERS
Scripting or running triggers is a series of programmed instructions that
is interpreted and carried out by the game rather than the player.  They can
be as simplistic as an eat/drink/anti-void trigger or they can be as complex
as a full leveling script.

You are allowed to run triggers or scripts as long as you are at your
keyboard AND attentive to the game.  This does not mean just to the imm
staff but to the entire game and the other people that may be trying to
interact with you.  If you are found to be away from your keyboard (AFK) for
a length of time and have any script or trigger running, EVEN IF YOU HAVE
THE AFK FLAG UP or you are found to not be paying attention to the game
after many attempts to get your attention have been ignored (and yes, that
includes you ignoring just to be an ass) you can and most likely will be
penalized.

Even the most basic trigger/script penalties are deliberately harsh to
discourage players from scripting as certain in game rewards evolve around
hours played.  The penalty may vary depending on the severity of the
incident though typically they will involve removal of hours as well as
other impacts to your character.  The more grevious offenses are outlined
below, will result in either a character dork or denial.  We also reserve
the right for imms to totally jack up your leveling scripts and send you to
your deaths.

If you are running a questing or leveling script and unresponsive - DENIAL

If you are script farming for gear, money or items for money - DENIAL

If you are craft scripting and unresponsive (1st offense) - CRAFT SKILL SET
TO 0

If you are craft scripting and unresponsive (2nd offense) - DORK

If you are penalized and wish to appeal your penalty, please send an email
of explanation to scorn@dsl-mud.  Org and to kyri@dsl-mud.  Org.  Be sure to
include the name of the character and DSL in the subject line.
