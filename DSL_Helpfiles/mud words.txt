'MUD WORDS'
As with most hobbyists, folks who mud often have terms that are common only
amongst others who engage in the same hobby.  For mudders, some of their
special vocabulary is common to computer users, some common to role-players,
and some common only to muds.  Some common mud-related words are listed below:

     Areas        Collections of interconnected rooms with a common theme
     Argument     Variable which follow a command
     IC           In character
     IP           Internet protocol, the numerical address of an internet site
     IRL          In real life
     Lowbie       A low level character of an experienced player
     Newbie       A player whos relatively new to the game or specific mud
     OLC          On Line Creation program for creating objects, mobs, and areas
     OOC          Out of character
     Roll         Generate a random number, derived from rolling dice in early rp games
     Rooms        Locations in a mud, you can see what happens in the room you are in
     RP           Role play
     THAC0        Your characters ability To Hit Armor Class zero
     Toggle       Repeating an action to turn something on and off
