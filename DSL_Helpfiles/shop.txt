SHOP SHOPS PLAYER MERCHANT
SHOPS - PLAYER OWNED SHOPS

Players can rent Merchants inside of a Kingdom's Merchant District.  There
is a one time fee of 25 eggs and a 1 egg per 30 days rental fee.

On these Merchants, players can sell in games items such as crafted items or
anything they get off of mobs.  A few items are restricted such as quest
items.

To purchase a merchant, please email Cayenna and include the kingdom and
open shop address you wish to purchase in.  Also have your descriptions for
your shop and shop keeper in the note.  You also require permission from the
leader of the kingdom where you wish to purchase a shop.  This can be send
as a note.

Shop Keeper Requirements:

For a proper shop keeper, we require a minimum of keywords (name), an
interaction description (short), and an in room description (long).  You may
also include sex, race, and a more detailed description (description).

Examples:

      Name(s) of Mob: Biff and/or Shopkeeper

     Short Example 1: Biff
     Short Example 2: a shopkeeper

      Long Example 1: Biff stands here waiting on customers.
      Long Example 2: A shopkeeper stands here.

         Description: A broad muscular man is here admiring his muscles
                      between helping customers.

Things you may not include are special greetings or actions your shopkeeper
will say or do when people enter the room.


Pricing:

To pay your rent on these merchants, type 'shopstatus pay'.  The merchant
will note you 7 days before your payment is due and then again 1 day before.
If you fail to make your rent, the merchant will quit and leave your items
on the ground 7 days after payment is past due.

     New Shop Cost -                         25 Eggs
     Re-Open Shop Cost -                     25 Eggs
     Shop Edit -                              5 Eggs Per Shop
     Extendeds -                              5 Eggs Per Extended
     Monthly Shop Cost -                      1 Egg  Per Shop

Syntax for Multiple Shop Payments: Shopstatus pay 1, Shopstatus pay 2, etc.


Transferring Bulk Items to Shop Keeper:

To add items to your merchant, you simple give them items or use
bulktransfer.  To set prices on items, setprice <item> <number>
<blue/gold/silver>.  You may order your shop keepers to give you items or
drop items by using the order command.

     Syntax:  give <item> <shopkeeper name>
     Syntax:  bulktransfer <quantity> <item> <shopkeeper name>
     Syntax:  setprice <item> <amount> <blue/gold/silver>
     Syntax:  order <shopkeeper name> drop <quantity> <item>
     Syntax:  order <shopkeeper name> give <item> <your name>

You can also order your merchant to give you any gold or silver he has on
him.  DO NOT give your merchant gold or silver, you will lose it.  If gold
or silver is left with your merchant, once they hold enough to equal an egg,
the egg(s) will be auto-deposited into your bank account.
