points.  (bounty points are gotten by killing other clanners)

Currently, the following items are available by typing bp list at a bounty
merchant.

Stormwhisper (whip)
Nightwhisp (dagger)
Revelation (polearm)
Stoneshatter (axe)
Evenstar (flail)
Moondancer (staff)
Truestrike (mace)
Skyrender (spear)
