PALADINS

The Paladin serves the Knighthood of Gareth as the holiest of champions.
They are the epitome of the Knighthood's virtues and teachings, and are
charged with serving simultaneously as the Shield for the righteous, the
Lance to smite the wicked, and the Crown to guide the faithful.  The Paladin
is well equipped to stand toe-to-toe with the fiercest of foes, and granted
the ability to seek out even the most cunning of evils.  They can destroy
undead with a touch, and heal even the most grievous of wounds.  It is no
wonder that when one of these rare champions take the field, the faithful
find the heart to stand against the Darkness.

The path of the Holy Warrior is not for everyone, and only a select few are
accepted by the Generals of the Knighthood, but once given approval by the
Generals and the Gods of Good to represent as one of their Champions, the
Paladin is rewarded in their faith with a celestial warhorse from Nadrik.
The Paladin's ability to smite the wicked is greatly enhanced when mounted,
and they are likewise gifted with spiritual power, granting their prayers
equal potency to even the most skilled of magi.

Who can be a Paladin?

    CLASS:  Any Class
     RACE:  Humans Only
ALIGNMENT:  Lawful Good Only
     CLAN:  KNIGHTHOOD Only
