STABLES

Stables are available for purchase by any Clan or Kingdom.  For
purchasing a group stable, you get pretty much everything that
you need in one package deal.  Here's what you get:

1- One room for the stables
2- A stablehand.
3- Three mounts of varying size, tailored to meet the needs of each clan.

(Hit and Dam are NOT high, nor may your mounts fly or be something
out of reason.  Uncommon type mounts need to remain UNCOMMON)

Cost of Stables:  400 Eggs
