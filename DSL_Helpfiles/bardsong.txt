BARDSONG

Tunes of wonder spill from the lips of a bard
Though sung oft enough they must learn the verse
Many songs they can learn, some easy some hard
But in order to practice, they must 'rehearse'

Should a bard seek to learn more songs they would know
Seek out a fellow master of song in the realm
Perhaps they can explain how each song goes.
