PRIVATE HOME
{YPRIVATE HOME{x

Any mortal may purchase a private home in a housing district.  Race
restrictions on kingdoms also apply to the housing district of the
kingdom. You may not buy in a district you do not reside in, without
leadership approval.

Lairs can be built in most locations but MUST be outside of a kingdom's
city limits.  Remorts have lairs and cannot also have a private house.

No more than 4 rooms build maximum for your house or lair.
