A restring is the renaming of an object or feature.  This is done by an
immortal, and it's validity is up to the immortal's discretion and restring
guide.  Admin has the right to overrule an existing restring, at any time.

Description features must be appropriate for the race and gender you are
playing.  Professions must fit DSLs Rp and time period.  All remorts and
divines should have professions set automatically by Imms and will be
changed at no charge.  Both can contain color syntax, if desired.

{cRestring Guidelines:{x

These are all the aspects of a restring that you will need to have ready,
when you pray up. Short, long, and names are required for all restrings.
Extended descriptions are optional.

{C1. Short Description{x - What appears in inventory, when worn, or when striking.

     {yExample:{x say short a silvery sword with an engraved hilt

Note:  No capitals unless proper nouns.

{C2. Long Description{x - What appears when the item is on the ground or examined.

     {yExample:{x say long A silvery sword with an engraved hilt lies here.

{C3. Names{x -  Keywords to reference the item.

     {yExample:{x say names bright silvery sword hilt

Note:  You may add extra names, but one must match the item type.

{C4. Extended Descriptions{x - Additional details for specific parts.

     {yExample:{x say extended engraving The hilt is engraved with the name Scorn.

Note:  Restrings with over 5 extendeds will have an extra cost.

{cOther rules:{x

   - Descriptions should describe appearance only.
   - No personal/god/family names without prior approval.
   - Must fit DSL's RP and time period.
   - Item type must remain consistent (dagger stays a dagger, etc.).
   - Use limited color codes (no excessive "kender colors").
   - Syntax for color codes: short a {{wsilver{{x handled {{Dsword{{x
   - Use common sense - no intangible items )auras, energy, rotting flesh, etc.)

   - {rFleesets{x must indicate a person leaving the area from battle.
   - {rRecalls{x must indicate a person leaving for home/bind/recall.
   - {rPortals{x must indicate moving through a portal.  Shows on opening side.

{cProcess for Requesting Restrings:{x

   - Pray for the number of restrings you need.
   - Give the immortal 1 blue diamond per restring and for objects, 1 item
     at a time to avoid errors.
