'ATTACK SPELLS'

The attack spells are essentially powerful curses, fueled by divine energy
rather than magical power (like the combat spells are).  They are less
powerful than the combat spells, but can still deal out a stinging blow.
Mages and thieves cannot use this group.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The attack spell group consists of the following spells:

demonfire       a powerful, but very evil, spell
dispel evil     torments evil foes
dispel neutral  inflicts pain upon those outside of neutral faith
dispel good     calls down unholy power on good creatures
earthquake      brings the power of earth to bear against your foes
flamestrike     sends a column of flame from the heavens
heat metal      heats metal equipment to searing temperatures
ray of truth    sends forth a blinding ray of holy energy

This group is available to the following classes -
    BATTLERAGER CLERIC DRAGON DRUID GIANT
    PALADIN PRIEST SHADOWKNIGHT RANGER WARRIOR
