CLAN VOUCHERS

A clan voucher allows a clan to bring in a loner, non-clanned or retired
person into their fold.  Clans can opt to pay for vouchers out of their
clanned funds, through any gifted vouchers they have received through events
or they can choose to have a player pay for this out of their own pocket.

In order for a player to move directly into a clan without first being a
loner, does require a note from the clan leader giving permission for this
action.  Also, if payment is being made through the clan's bank account or
their gifted vouchers, this will also need a note from the clan leader and a
senior imm to complete the transaction.

Supervouchers are very rare but they may bring anyone into any clan
regardless of restrictions.  Also, players who are still in their base class
(have not yet reclassed) may loner for free until level 25.

Voucher Costs:

Non-Clan/Retired to Loner =   75 Eggs
 Non-Clan/Retired to Clan =   75 Eggs

            Clan to Loner =   50 Eggs
             Clan to Clan =   50 Eggs
        Renegade to Loner =   50 Eggs

            Supervouchers = 1000 Eggs


For Non-CSR's

Dwarves may change from another clan to Wargar for free.

Elves (except dark) may change from another clan to Shalonesti for free.

Mage and Mage reclasses (except Witch/Warlocks) may change from another
clan to Conclave for free.

Switching clans for non-CSR dwarves, elves and mages are free one-time only.
This does not apply to loners or renegades.


For CSR's

If you are a CSR seeking to return to the same clan that you earned your
CSR in, then you can voucher back in, with permission for leadership at the
cost of 75 eggs.  This will require an imm to guild.

If you are a CSR seeking another clan from where you earned your CSR, see
HELP SUPERVOUCHER.
