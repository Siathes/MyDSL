ELDRITCHES

With the impending threat of Chaos and the coming Wars, Zandreya bestowed
upon her chosen race the teachings needed to defend themselves against the
incoming onslaught.  It was through this cultivation that the Eldritch came
to be.  Borrowing a handful of spellbooks from both the cleric and the mage,
they are able to cast debilitating maledictions as well as summon
mountainous beasts.

Once fully trained in the basic arts, the Eldritch is able to choose from
one of three exclusive paths, the sun, the moon or the stars in which they
can draw energy from in order to inflict a more focused and powerful magic
upon their enemy.  Only after an abundance of time and mass training through
loyalty and dedication, may the Eldritch be able to seek a second path.

Who can be an Eldritch?

CLASS:     Any class
RACE:      Elves only
ALIGNMENT: Any alignment
CLAN:      SHALONESTI only
