IMPROVE IMPROVEMENT

syntax:    improve <skillname>
           improve none

The improve command allows you to train your skills and spells after you
have practiced them.  Normally, you would use this improvement from 75% to
100% of a skill or spell's ability.  It is possible to improve a skill or
spell if they are not practiced to 75%, but the improvement rate will be
much slower than if the skill or spell is already at or beyond 75%.

Skills and spells will continue to improve once you set them no matter what
you are doing at the time.  They will also improve while you are offline
(again, at a slower rate).  The overall rate of improvement is based upon
your intelligence and the difficulty of the skill or spell.

Typing improve by itself will show you what skill or spell you are currently
training if you are in the process of training one.
