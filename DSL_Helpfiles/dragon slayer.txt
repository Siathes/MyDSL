DRAGON SLAYER

The Dragon Slayer class is a warrior-like class designed specifically, over
decades of trial and error, to combat Dragons.  Dragon Slayers detest
Dragons and would rather kill one than to exchange two words with one.  They
hate dragons of all alignments and tend to adorn their own armor with the
skin of defeated dragons.  It is said that a Dragon Slayer never quite
quenches his thirst for dragon blood, but rather grows in strength and
determination with each dragon they slay.

This class is limited to that of the Slayer clan and can only be obtained
there.

Who can be a Dragon Slayer?

    CLASS: Any Class
     RACE: Any Race
ALIGNMENT: Any Alignment
     CLAN: SLAYERS only
