[ Loner ]

Loners are part of the clan system, able to kill and steal from other
players, but have not decided to make close associations with an organized
clan.  Loners can go loner for free from levels 5 - 25 but must have a
voucher (which can be purchased) to clan after level 25.  Loners have no
safehavens, unless they were wealthy enough to purchase a private home.  If
one wishes to go loner, they can do so by finding the nearest trainer and
seeking his aid in doing so:

{rNOTE:{x} All characters MUST have an adequate description before they loner,
regardless of level.
