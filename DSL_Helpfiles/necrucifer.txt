NECRUCIFER

After the birth of the land, Necrucifer, one of the chosen, was given
tertiary rule by the High God.  The Father of Darkness was never born,
rather eternal: always was, always will be.

In an attempt to seize the world, Necrucifier sought to assassinate
Austinian, the Father of Goodness.  Their battle spanned the heavens and
land alike, carrying from the immortal stars to the lands below.  Each
struck blows to one another, causing their immortal blood to flow freely
upon the land.  The blood of Necrucifer tainted the rivers and oceans, its
very essence permeated everywhere in the land, spawning evil creatures and
beings alike.

Legends state that when Necrucifer is upset the stars will fall from the
sky, leaving the world in utter and total darkness.  Some myths also state
that he will return to the land to take it as his own, slaying both
Austinian and Kwainin.

When he appears, rumor has it that he wears a dark cowl that perpetually
covers his face, and will not walk into any lighted areas, rather, he
prefers darkness.  If he has no other choice and he _needs_ to enter a
lighted area, he has been known to extinguish all natural and unnatural
lights, so he may bathe in the darkness.

Necrucifer coupled with Drakkara to birth his children: Fatale, Dragoth,
Devion and Mencius.  All of them together form the Sphere of Darkness, which
is ultimately ruled by Necrucifer.
