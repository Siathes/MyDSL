MOBGOD INTERACTION

This helpfile deals mainly with priests, but its principles can be applied
to any class.

In order to ensure a fair and accurate system for divine/mortal interaction,
it is encouraged for serious priests to use a website to track interaction
with their god.  Rather than creating a background, simply place a link to
your website in place of your background.  Post information about yourself,
interaction that takes place between you and your god, notes, storynotes,
and any other pertinent information you would like your god to know about.
Simple links to unedited log files would be just fine, but you can get as
fancy as you would like.  There are numerous free websites available for
your use.  The following are just some examples of where to find them:

http://www.tripod.lycos.com/
http://geocities.yahoo.com/

Regardless of your rank or level as a priest, communicating with your god
will always help your chances of interaction and promotion.  Write notes,
send up gprays - but dont send up the same old tired gprays over and over.

If you have any issues or a crisis that needs to be handled, first try to
resolve it IC through your church leader.  If that doesnt work, the RP
Department has the overall responsibility for ensuring consistency of mobgod
interaction.  Address any complaints to the RP Director(s) and staff and
they will answer any questions or concerns you might have.
