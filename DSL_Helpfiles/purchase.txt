PURCHASE
Jewelled eggs, the most valuable possession in the land, can now be
bought from most shopkeepers.  To buy a jewelled egg from a shopkeeper,
please have 50 diamonds or 10 blue diamonds in a bag, with nothing else in
the bag.  Then, simply type 'purchase', and the shopkeeper will slip the
jewelled egg into your inventory.  If you have more than required change will
now be given.
Please do not use the pray channel to ask for jewelled eggs anymore.
