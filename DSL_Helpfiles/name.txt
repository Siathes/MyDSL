WELCOME TO DARK AND SHATTERED LANDS (DSL)
At DSL, we insist on a roleplaying enviroment.  To do so, we do not allow
you to use any name you want.  The names MUST meet our preset
criteria.  If they do not, you will be renamed or more likely denied.
Make sure the name is of a medieval nature. Also make sure it isn't a
combination of two other names. (example: JohnPaul) Also be sure that
the name is not found in the dictionary or is a common slang term.
