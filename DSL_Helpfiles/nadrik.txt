NADRIK
Nadrik, son of Austinian and Taliena, is the sworn upholder of Honor and
sworn enemy of Devion. Legends report that Nadrik was lured into battle
by Devion, a battle he surely won, when he severed the hand of Devion,
forcing him to flee.
After the battle, he learned of Devion's plans to capture his sister
Siccara and helped her avoid his sinister plot.
When Nadrik graces the realm, he usually does so with fanfare.
Oftentimes, he has been known to visit the land with his sister Siccara,
as they are as close as brother and sister can hope to be.
He appears dressed down in regal, polished armor, always prepared to
draw his weapon at the first signs of Honor breached.
