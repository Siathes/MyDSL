RECLASS RECLASSES

 Reclassing is a somewhat unique option available to all players on DSL. A
 reclass is simply that: A new class.  Often, adventurers find that they
 wish to either narrow or broaden their profession.  The mage may wish to
 focus on necromancy, the warrior may wish to broaden his skills.  To this
 end, DSL has a number of reclasses.  To see which reclasses your character
 qualifies for, see the individual help files.

 Some common questions about reclasses:
  Q. What is the minimum level to reclass?
  {YA{x. 10.
  Q. How will reclassing affect my chances of being a manatonic?
  {YA{x. It has no bearing on your manatonic rolls or potential.
  Q. What if I already *am* manatonic?  Do I lose it?
  {YA{x. No, your powers will remain as they are.
  Q. Do I have to be clanned?
  {YA{x. That depends on the reclass.  Read up on the one of interest.
  Q. When is the best time to reclass?
  {YA{x. You will get the most benefit if you do it at level 51.
  Q. I've got a neat idea for a new reclass.  Can we try it?
  {YA{x. Maybe.  Email Scorn at scorn@dsl-mud.org and find out.
  Q. How many times can I reclass?
  {YA{x. Just once, so make sure you like what you picked.
  Q. Can I reclass and remort?
  {YA{x. Yep.  You may do each once, so long as you reclass first.
  Q. What happens to my strength and other attributes?
  {YA{x. They remain unchanged.

 When you reclass, the following things happen:
  * Your maximum hit points, mana, and moves are halved.
  * Your level is halved.
  * Your hours are halved.
  * Any saved practices and trains are halved, then added to.
  * You will gain practices:
       The practices added will vary between 13 and 43 depending on level.
  * You will be put back into the creation mode, on the skill selection
    screen.  From here, you may select all new skills and groups for your
    character.  Depending on the severity of the change, you may find that
    some of your old skills cannot be re-selected, but new ones may also
    be available.  You are free to pick and choose whatever is available.
    Keep in mind that a 3 creation point penalty will applied once you are
    done, and so your experience per level requirement will increase
    somewhat when you are done.
  * Once done, your character will be given his/her new class, and
    transferred to his/her recall point (home town).
  * Any skill that you _originally_ had, and took again when you
    reclassed:
        + will start at 1% if it's below 75% before you reclass.
        + will start at your adept level (75% for most) if it's 75% or
          higher.

 Reclasses listed with an * next to the name are not yet available.

 For more information on any specific reclass, including what base classes
 and races a character must be for that reclass, type help <reclass name>.

 To find out what skills and spells are included in the defaults for a
 reclass use INFO <reclass name>.
