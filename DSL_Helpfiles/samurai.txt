SAMURAI

Samurai are warriors hailing from the continent of Shokono.  They serve the
noble lords of these lands as their military elite and retainers.  Sworn to
the bushido code, Samurai are masters of various forms of combat, both
mounted and on foot.  It is rumored that the Samurais affinity for swords is
nearly unmatched, and that this is due to their devotion to their Code.
These rumors also state that one would be lucky to encounter a samurai on
foot, for their skill with bows upon a horse rarely leaves such an
opportunity.

Being noble retainers, The Samurai are sworn military leaders, and as such
their devotion and service will gain them renown with their lords. This
renown takes the form of a rank structure given specifically to the samurai.
These ranks are Ronin, Samurai, and Shogun.  To the untrained eye, these
ranks are nearly indistinguishable, but it is rumored that the Samurai grows
in his abilities from this renown, and perhaps the best indicator of these
rumors, are the pets they train.

Who can be a Samurai?
CLASS: WARRIORS only
RACE: All Except Goblins
ALIGNMENT: Any alignment
CLAN: ANY, including non-clanned
