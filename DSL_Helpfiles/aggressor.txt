AGGRESSOR

An 'aggressor' flag is a rare flag that will be flagged on any player who is
attempting to attack a giant.  This flag is similar to the Hostile flag in
the sense that it allows the giant to defend themselves against Kingdomers
who have attacked them.  The rules with kingdom/clan interference still
apply.

Please note this is also a flag that can be set by the Immortals, at their
discretion, and can be used in a variety of situations such as with taunting
between kingdomers and clanner and/or remorts where things become heated,
annoying or twinkish.  Any player marked with the aggressor flag is on their
own and can be killed.  Be wary of this flag and know any abuse will NOT be
tolerated.

The ability for players to 'pour' to activate this flag, is no longer
active.
