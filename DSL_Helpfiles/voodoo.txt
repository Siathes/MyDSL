VOODOO
'VOODOO SPELLS'

This batch of ancient chants and spirit magic is the stock in trade of the
witch-doctor, or shaman.  These spells are of a realm that no other class
can make use of.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The voodoo spell group consists of the following spells:

leprosy         inflicts target with a flesh eating disease
wither          reduces target to a doddering old fool
soulsight       allows shaman to see magicaly cloaked people
spiritwalk      allows the shaman to become a spirit and walk the lands
beastform       the shaman dons the form of a beast
thunderclap     creates a large deafening thunderclap in the area
snakebite       creates a hightly venomous snake which bites the target
poultice        basic form of healing
shrink head     allows shaman to shrink the head of a fallen enemy

This group is available to the following classes -
    SHAMAN
