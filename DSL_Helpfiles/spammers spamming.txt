SPAMMERS SPAMMING

Spam is when you repeatedly type in the same thing.  While most forms of
spam are harmless (i.e. spamming 'east' to reach New Thalos), people will
always abuse things.

Channels are the most commonly spammed parts of muds, since that will
provide the IMMS and players with the most annoyance.

You are allowed to input the same command up to 10 times.  This allows
people to repeatedly cast spells to improve their skill level, spam in
directions, or spam in certain skills to improve them.  This use of spamming
is currently being permitted.  However, if you plan on spamming to raise
your skill/spell levels, make sure that you are at the keyboard and paying
attention.  Players found running a script while away from the keyboard will
be punished.

It may be changed in the future, so watch for any notices regarding the
issue.  If you spam more than 34 times, the game will drop your link and
give you a lovely message about it.

Imms may step in before 10 spams if you are being a nuisance (i.e. spamming
'hello' in a commonly used room, or spamming public channels).  Spam at your
own risk.  Different IMMs will have different opinions on the matter.
