'WHITE ROBES'
[White Robes]

The White Robes is a clan of mages that practice the art of magic whilst
trying to retain a pure heart, untainted by the ever-present touch of evil.
The mages of the White reside in the White Tower of Kantilles.

These magi must rely on their magical aptitude in order to defend themselves
from their enemies.  They also rely on such to further the teaching of magic
within the realm, as one of the towers that make up the entirety that is the
Conclave.
