IMPOSTER
{YIMPOSTER{x

{cSyntax:{x cast 'imposter' <player name/mob name>

Imposter is an Illusionist spell that allows the user to take the form of
another player.  Use 'revert' to revert to your original self.  Caution is
advised however, because the transition from a morphed form to your original
form has been known to be tough to do.

{rNOTE:{x It is illegal for clanners to imposter kingdomers to get around keep
code rules.  Kingdomers cannot enter keeps so guard code does not recognize
them and would be an example of bypassing code.
