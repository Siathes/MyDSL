keeps
There are 3 keeps in which clanners may claim and own.

Thorn Keep - located upon Shokono

Clanners may enter the Keep and if they can kill the Lord, the keep will
belong to them until taken by another clan.  Owning the Keep means that the
clan gets portal access to Thorn Keep on Shokono for quick defending and a
Thorn Aura spell from their clans healer that returns slight damage when
hit.

Misery Keep - located upon Tropica

This Keep is similar to Thorn Keep, with the exception that the clan who
holds this Keep will get to cast a Vale of Misery spell on an enemy clan,
giving everyone in that clan an AC penalty.

Syntax: Setmisery <clan>/none (only a Leader may set this)

* WARNING: setting it to none will take away ownership of the Keep

The Citadel Keep - located on Icewall

This Keep is similar to the others with the exception that it gives the
owning clan a Saves bonus.

There is nothing illegal about killing the mobs within the Keeps once you
own them, but it is to your own clan's detriment if; you do, as the guards
are there to aide in its defense for you.  If you own the Keep and kill the
Lord again, ownership will revert to being unowned.
