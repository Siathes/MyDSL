{YMILL MILLER{x

This is a 2nd tier craft that requires the miller to get their logs from
another player after it has been harvested.  Pricing in acquiring the logs
can vary as this is set by the individual crafters. Milling requires you be
in a room with an enchanted sawmill.  These are quite expensive and likely
only found in kingdom halls or clan halls.  You will also require the
correct type of log to cut the boards you wish to make.

      {cSyntax:{x mill <wood type>

{yWood Types{x - Cedar, Fir, Pine, Elm, Oak, Maple, Hickory, Stonewood,
            Ironwood, Dreadwood

{rNote:{x that if you have an alt that is a lumberjack and mills, you cannot
use material gained from one in order to benefit the other.  See
multiplaying rules.


{DSEE ALSO:{x} HARVESTWOOD LUMBERJACK CRAFT CRAFTING
