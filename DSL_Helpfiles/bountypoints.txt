BOUNTYPOINTS
The amount of loots you get for killing a player depend on the amount of
bountypoints you have.

  1 -  49bp: 1 loot
 50 -  99bp: 2 loots
100 - 199bp: 3 loots
199 - 299bp: 4 loots
299 - 399bp: 5 loots
399 - 499bp: 6 loots
499 - 599bp: 7 loots
599 - 699bp: 8 loots
699 - 799bp: 9 loots
799 - 899bp: 10 loots
899 - 999bp: 11 loots
1000+      : full loot
