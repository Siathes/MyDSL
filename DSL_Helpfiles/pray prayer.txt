PRAY PRAYER

DO NOT, DO NOT, DO NOT spam this channel please.  It is very easy to lose
this channel for misuse.

Usage:

You may use this channel to request things such as reporting someone
cheating, asking to be guilded, or asking questions of relevance to
immortals.  Please specify your need in your prayer.  Prayers of 'Can anyone
help me with a question in regard to how sea-elves should be RPd' will
likely be answered more quickly than prayers of 'Help'or, 'Are there any
level 58s on'.  You may also use it in a roleplaying sense but do so only on
occasion.

(Example: pray Lord Austinian, guide me on this quest if you would)

Some immortals can get very annoyed at someone spamming the channel.

Once in a 1/2 hour is plenty, it is not a chat line to the immortals.  Treat
it as such and you will lose it.  Do not get too upset if you lose your pray
privileges for a while, it is very easy to lose and that may be the best way
to learn for some.

Also, priests who have trained themselves to maximum capacity may make use
of priest specific praying abilities by using gpray instead of pray.

(see help gpray, and help priest)
