INVITE UNINVITE UN-INVITE
Syntax: invite
Syntax: invite <character>
Syntax: uninvite <character>
INVITE is used to invite a player (allow) into all the rooms that you own,
I.E Town Homes. You MUST be the owner of the room.
INVITE with nothing after give you a list of all the people you've invited.
UNINVITE is used to uninvite a player (disallow) that you previously invited.
Note, Invitation info is not saved.
