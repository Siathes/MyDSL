'NECROMANCY SPELLS'

The necromancy school of magic consists of all spells dealing with death,
the dead, and the undead.  It not a study of how to kill, merely what to do
with the powers surrounding the termination of life.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The necromancy spell group consists of the following spells:

embalm             mummifies corpses and body parts
locate remains     locates the corpse of player or mob
graft flesh        grafts multiple corpse parts into an animated beast
corpse host        caster becomes hosted in the corpse of an undead body
death shroud       extremely powerful protection spell
empath             transfers the wounds of someone else onto the caster
feign death        caster assumes the appearance of a corpse
scourge            a lethal disease....permanent until cured or death occurs
bone blight        a disease inflicting a weakness apon the target's bones
shadowform         caster become undetectable in all but brightest light
animate dead       raises a corpse into an undead servant of varying power
detect undead      detects the presence of undead creatures
energy drain       drains mana and movement from target
withstand death    caster is able to withstand death

This group is available to the following classes -
    NECROMANCER
