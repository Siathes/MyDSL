kmutiny
Kingdom Mutiny System

There are two ways to mutiny leadership over a kingdom.

1: Collect an army through the kingdom wars system and attack the kingdom
and its army until victory or surrender.

2: Call for a revolt of the people!  If the citizens of the kingdom are
unhappy enough, they will over throw the crown and help you obtain the
crown!

a: To call for a revolt, you must first obtain the permission of your
kingdoms watcher.  Be warned, you must have a good penalty history and have
a GREAT reason for wanting to steal power.  Be prepared to talk your watcher
into allowjng you the chance.

b: If approved, a vote of citizens will commence.  You need to obtain the
vote a 3/5 of the kingdoms population to steal the crown.  Any member of the
kingdom with 100 or more prestige hours are eligible to vote.
