LOOT LOOTING

When an enemy clanner (or individual in the clanned system) is killed, it is
possible to loot one or more items from them.  The number of items that can
be looted is dependent upon how many bounty points you have.  Please note,
containers are considered one item, regardless of whether they have many
things within them.

There are rules that need to be followed when you kill an enemy, regardless
of whether you are clanned are not.  They are as follows.

DO NOT sac any of the enemy's gear.
DO NOT sac their floating items.
DO NOT drag gear away (whether you're on their side or not).
DO NOT drag an enemy corpse away.
DO NOT bury an enemy's gear.
DO NOT tornado an enemy's gear.
DO NOT guard the corpse so that you can kill the newly unghosted enemy.

YOU MUST tell the person where they died if they do not know.
              (help rules, 2b)
