TMOTE
TMOTE (Targeted Emote)

Syntax: tmote target phrase

TMOTE QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE
===========================
Lower case TMOTE directs toward target. (eg. @m & @mf)
Upper case TMOTE directs toward poster. (eg. @M & @Mf)

        Others See                  Directed Toward Sees
        -----------------------     --------------------
@m      him/her/it                  you
@mf     himself/herself/itself      yourself
@s   *  his/her/its                 your
@ss     his/her/its                 yours
@e   *  he/she/it                   you
@t   *  Subject                     you
@p   *  Subject's                   your
@ps     Subject's                   yours
@hi     is                          are
@hh     has                         have
@hw     was                         were
@v      verb +s                     verb
@x      verb +es                    verb
@y      verb ending in y to ies     verb ends in y

* Auto-capitalizes after end of sentence on POSTER TMOTE option
  only (@S, @E, @T, and @P).


TMOTE DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS
===========================
All TMOTE commands start with the @ symbol.

You must have one @ tmote command in the phrase
(preferably at least one @t so the target is identified)

Using a Dependent Clause within TMOTE
-------------------------------------
Your tmote phrase must begin with a comma.
The dependent clause must have a closing comma.

  eg: tmote player ,Leaning against the wall, looks at @t.
  becomes: Leaning against the wall, Xandor looks at you.

Please note you may not use the tmote options below in the dependent clause.

TMOTE Target commands
---------------------
TMOTE target commands are directed to the person you listed as
your target in the command.  They will see something different than what
you and the room will see.

Subject specific TMOTES:

        Poster/Room sees           Target sees
        Target as                  Themself as
        ----------------------     -----------
@m      him/her/it                 you
@mf     himself/herself/itself     yourself
@s      his/her/its                your
@ss     his/her/its                yours
@e      he/she/it                  you
@t      Target                     you
@p      Target's                   your
@ps     Target's                   yours

Helper Verbs:
The letter after @h indicates helper verbs that the target sees
differently than you or the room do.

        Poster/Room sees           Target sees
        Target as                  Themself as
        ----------------           -----------
@hi     is                         are
@hh     has                        have
@hw     was                        were

Verb TMOTES:
The 3 verb options allow verbs to be tensed correctly within TMOTE
toward the target. You put the TMOTE verb command directly before the verb.

@v  Poster and Room see s on the end of a verb.
    Target does not see s on the end of the verb.
@x  Poster and Room see es on the end of a verb.
    Target does not see es on the end of the verb.
@y  Poster and Room see ies on the end of a verb that ends in y.
    Target sees the y on the end of the verb.

    eg: @vemote
    eg: @xscratch
    eg: @yparty

    Poster/Room sees     Target sees
    verb as              verb as
    ----------------     -----------
    emotes               emote
    scratches            scratch
    parties              party

TMOTE Poster Commands
---------------------
TMOTE Poster commands are directed towards you.  You will see something different
than what the target and the room will see.  They are identical to the Target
TMOTES, but are uppercase

Subject specific TMOTES:

        Target/Room sees            Poster sees
        Poster as                   themself as
        ----------------------      -----------
@M      him/her/it                  you
@Mf     himself/herself/itself      yourself
@S   *  his/her/its                 your
@Ss     his/her/its                 yours
@E   *  he/she/it                   you
@T   *  Poster                      you
@P   *  Poster's                    your
@Ps     Poster's                    yours

     * Will automatically capitalize after a period
       and either one or two spaces.

Helper Verb TMOTES:
The letter after @H indicates helper verbs that you see
differently than the target or the room do.

        Target/Room sees            Poster sees
        Poster as                   themself as
        ----------------            -----------
@Hi     is                          are
@Hh     has                         have
@Hw     was                         were

Verb TMOTES:
The 3 verb options allow verbs to be tensed correctly within TMOTE
toward you.  You put the TMOTE verb command directly before the verb.

@V  Target and Room see s on end of verb.
    Poster does not see s on end of verb.
@X  Target and Room see es on the end of a verb.
    Poster does not see es on the end of the verb.
@Y  Target and Room see ies on the end of a verb that ends in y.
    Poster sees the y on the end of the verb.

    eg: @Vemote
    eg: @Xscratch
    eg: @Yparty

    Target/Room sees     Poster sees
    verb as              verb as
    ----------------     -----------
    emotes               emote
    scratches            scratch
    parties              party

Please note: With TMOTE the poster will see you instead of the poster's
    character name so you will want to use @V, @X, and @Y on many of your
    verbs so the tense shows correctly.
