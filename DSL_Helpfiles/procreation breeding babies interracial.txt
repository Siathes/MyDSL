only.  All other races can only create offspring from the same race, except
muls which are sterile and can not make children EVER!

NOTE: This is Ogre only, NOT Giant Ogre  All other races can ONLY breed
with other like races.

NOTE:  Remorts do not produce offspring with any mortal.  Dragon alterforms
can produce offspring but the child(ren) will not have any dragon qualities
and will need to follow the procreation chart for appropriate race.

Example: Hill Dwarves with Hill Dwarves, Mountain Dwarves with Mountain
Dwarves, Bugbears with Bugbears and so on and so forth.  Humans and Dwarves of
any type, DO NOT make Muls.
