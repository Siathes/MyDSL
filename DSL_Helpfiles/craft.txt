CRAFT
This is used by all top tier crafters to create items for player use.  You
may do this anywhere but you are required to have all the items needed for
the item you are trying to create.  For some crafts like weapon making you
will have a list of trinkets that you can craft to hone your skills.  You
can sell these trinkets to the merchants that sell knives, saws, and
pickaxes to cut your costs.

Syntax: craft <skill> <item>

Example: craft sharpweapon 'long bronze trinket'

Skills:
sharpweapon  - makes sharp weapons axes, swords, daggers, spears, and polearms
bluntweapon  - makes blunt weapons maces, flails, staffs, whips, and exotics
armorcraft   - makes leather studded, chainmail, and platemail armor
tailor       - makes cloth and leather armor
spellcrafter - makes gems to imbue onto armor and weapons
