LEVELING

How to Level -


MOB's (aka Monsters/Creatures):

You gain experience for killing monsters and NPCs, also referred to as
MOB's.  To check the relative strength of the MOB, type consider <target>.

If you think you can defeat the MOB, type kill <target> to engage  it.  If
there are multiple targets in the room with the same name,  type kill #
<target> instead.  So if you want to kill the third man listed in the room,
you would type kill 3.man.

You can cast a spell by typing cast <'spell'> <target>.  If you wanted to
cast 'shocking grasp' on a fish, it would be cast 'shocking grasp' fish.

Experience:

The experience rewarded after defeating a MOB depends on three things: the
relative level of the MOB compared to your own, the  alignment of the MOB
compared to yours, and the amount of hours you have.

If you kill MOB's of the same-alignment, you'll receive less experience.
However, if you kill MOB's of the opposing alignment, you will receive more
experience.  If you are neutral,  then you will receive average experience
from all MOB's, as as would a good/evil-aligned character killing neutral
MOBs.

Hours:

Hours affect your experience in multiples of your level.  If you are level
10 and have 10 hours, you will receive the normal amount of experience.  If
you are level 10 and have 20 hours, you will receive  double experience.
Finally, at level 10 with 30 hours, you will  receive the maximum amount of
experience.  If you have less than 10  hours at level 10, you will receive
less experience.

Don't try to rush your way through levels.  Spend some time exploring the
areas and meeting people instead.

Training:

The first area you should try to train in is the MUD School's cage room

From Althainia's recall, walk 2s, 6e, u.  Go all north, then  down, then
type scan to check the surrounding rooms for MOB's.

Type HELP NEWBIE to return to the table of contents.
