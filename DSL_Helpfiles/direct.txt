Syntax:  direct <name> <message>
         > <name> <message>
This command is for use when in a room with several people, and you wish
to say something, but have it directed to a particular person.  If someone
directs something they say towards you, you will see:  Scorn says (to you)
'message'.  If something is directed towards another character you will see
Scorn says (to Jinx) 'message'.
WARNING:  The DIRT KICKING command loads before the DIRECT command.  If you
abbreviate this to "dir" and have the dirt kicking skill, you will be
attacking the person to whom you wish to direct a communication.  If you
don't want to start a fight, use the > form or type at least "dire".
