ARMOR CLASS

Armor Class is a measure of how well you are protected from various types of
attacks.  Lower (more negative) numbers indicate that you are better
protected from attack, though you won't see numerical values for your armor
class until you reach a certain level.

Each character has 4 armor classes which relate to how well he/she is
protected against: Bashing, Slashing, Piercing, and Magical attacks.

The following classes of armor are restricted to the following classes:

Cloth:

Mage, Illusionist, Enchantor, Priest, Necromancer, Invoker, Transmuter,
Monk, Runesmith, Eldritch, Battlemage, Shadowmage, WuJen

Leather:

Jongleur, Witch/Warlock, Shaman, Druid, Cleric, Nightshade, Pirate,
Confessor, Ninja

Studded Leather:

Ranger, Swashbuckler, Assassin, Charlatan, Thief, Bard, Bandit,
Brewmaster

Chain Mail:

Crusader, Bladesinger, Anti-Paladin, Skald, Shukenja

Plate Mail:

Warrior, Barbarian, Battlerager, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Dragon Slayer,
Samurai
