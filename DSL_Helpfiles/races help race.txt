Limited Races -

            Trolls            Gully Dwarves           Ariels
            Pixies            Centaurs                Orcs

Different races have different starting statistics, and also different
stat maximums for magic items and training.

Creation points increase the amount of experience it takes to gain a level.

For more information, see the help files for each race (i.e. 'help Ogre').

Other races you may see on DSL are remorts.  Remorts are given to players
by Scorn through different means and are 100% IC AT ALL TIMES.  The only
exception to this is when you are alone with a member of the Imm staff to
discuss any issue or to ask questions.  The currently available remorts are:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Bronze Dragon      Copper Dragon      Steel Dragon

    Green Dragon       White Dragon       Brown Dragon

    Archangel          Lesser Angel
