AFK

Syntax: AFK - Typing AFK again will remove the flag

This command is used to signal other players that you are AFK (Away From
your Keyboard).  Typing AFK will place the flag on your character and by
typing it again, it will remove the flag.  Typing AFK puts your character in
a tell-saving mode, placing any tell to your character in a special buffer
that can be retrieved upon your return by removing the AFK flag and by
typing This is useful when you need to leave your computer for 5 - 10
minutes but do not want to miss any tell sent to you.  AFK will show in your
prompt until it is turned off.

Tells to link-dead players are stored in a similar fashion, and can also be
seen by typing replay (unless, of course, the character logs off before
reading them).  In the event of a reboot or crash while you are AFK, your
tells will be lost.

Please note: You will not gain hours or improve spells and/or skills while
you are in AFK mode.  Further note that any running of scripts or doing
anything that will keep you in the game when you are not at your keyboard
(or paying attention to the game including being on tabbed screens), is
ILLEGAL.
