AUSTINIAN
After the High God created the land, Austinian became one of this
world's protectors, the champion of everything good and pure.  He, like
Kwainin and Necrucifer is eternal, never being born, never able to die.
Legends consistenly represent Austinian as a young man in appearance
with long brown hair tied in braids down his back.  His eyes are of the
stars, silver and kind.  It has been reported by prophets and witnesses
that when Austinian enters the realm the goodness and innocent of the
world perform far beyond their capabilities.  A gentle myth of Proleit,
a farmer mentions how when Austinian graced him, his farm grew more
plentiful than it ever had before.
His devoted and faithful wife Taliena birthed him two sons and two
daughters: Kantilles, Nadrik, Siccara and Kadiya.  Together, as a
family, they compromise the sphere of goodness.
