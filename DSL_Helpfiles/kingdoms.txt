KINGDOMS

Althainian Continent:

   Althainia  - The leading economic Kingdom in the world.  Traditionally
                powerful and good in nature.

   New Thalos - A Sultan and Sultana lead these people with Arabian
                flavor.  This is a traditionally neutral kingdom.

   Shalonesti - The Elven Kingdom of legend.  Always a Shalonesti Elf as
                King or Queen from House Shalonost.

   Thaxanos   - The Dwarven Kingdom from northern Althainia.

   Abaddon    - The ancient Undead Kingdom ruled by the Vampire Queens.


Arkania Continent:

   Arkane     - The mystical Kingdom of western Arkania.  Traditionally
                a neutral kingdom.

   Verminasia - The famous evil Kingdom on Arkania.

   Marauders  - A group of conquerors out to defeat any kingdom they see
                fit.  Traditionally a neutral to evil group.

Icewall Continent:

   Ganth      - The Minotaur Kingdom on the continent of Icewall.

   Nordmaar   - The Celtic Kingdom located on southern Icewall.

   Darkonin   - The Goblin and Ogre Kingdom located in Icewall.


Type HELP <KINGDOM NAME> for more specific information on that kingdom.
