BLOODBATH (BB) RULES

Bloodbaths, more commonly known as BB's are quests run by immortals.
Players are transferred into an arena and after preparing are let out to
fight each other. The arenas are set up so that you do not lose eq
rot_death items, or any such things affected by death except for experience.

Dragons, Alterforms, Divines and Giants are not allowed to participate.

Type bbsetup for further instructions on how to make one.

1  No tornado
2  No teleport
3  No vanish
4  No heat metal
5  No steal
6  No violent disposition
7  No following (To group you must follow, so no grouping)
8  No shockers
9  No excessive use of non-detects
10 No brewing in the prep area
11 Any additional rules the imm running it may apply.
12 All Charmies are only gotten once inside the arena not the prep room.

The same rules apply to ANY BB, that includes the automated one.
The ONLY exception is an imm run one where they are there and they
make it non-standardized (ie no spells, no weapons, etc.)
