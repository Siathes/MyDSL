ARKANE

On the western point of the continent of Arkania lies the kingdom of Arkane.
Arkane is known throughout Algoron as the kingdom of rare and mystical
powers, where some of the finest magi are first found.  Arkane is a center
of trade given its proximity to a nearby seaport.

Arkane is traditionally a neutral kingdom that welcomes all who come to
study, learn and add to its mystical traditions.  It's focus and power are
maintained by an active monarchy bent on protecting its longstanding
traditions.
