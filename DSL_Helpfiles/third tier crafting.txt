THIRD TIER CRAFTING' '3rd TIER CRAFTING'

Apprentices of the 3rd tier create finished items such as weapons, armor
and other various equipment for player use.  Spellcrafting varies slightly
from the others and all are covered in separate help files. You may craft
anywhere but you are required to have all the items in your inventory
needed for the item you are trying to create.

All 3rd tier crafts require that you first master the making of a trinket
of a particular material or in the case of spellcrafting, a gemstone,
before you can create the weapons, armor or gems of that same material.
Every craft has a skill level range that you need to achieve in order
to created the items within that range.

You can sell all crafted trinkets back to the merchants that sell the
materials needed for any craft.  The price that you get for the trinkets
on resale depends on the type of material used.

Skills-

sharpweapon  - makes sharp weapons axes, swords, daggers, spears, and polearms
bluntweapon  - makes blunt weapons maces, flails, staffs, whips, and exotics
armorcraft   - makes leather studded, chainmail, and platemail armor
tailor       - makes cloth and leather armor
spellcrafter - makes gems to imbue onto armor and weapons

Note that if you have an alt that is a 1st or 2nd tier crafter and you
use  materials created by them for your alt that is a 3rd tier crafter, it
is illegal.  You cannot use material gained from one in order to benefit
the other.  See multiplaying rules.
