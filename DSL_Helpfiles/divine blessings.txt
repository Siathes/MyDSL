DIVINE BLESSINGS

The spells of divine blessing are restricted to the priests of the realm.
Only those truly devout are given the right to cast these blessings that are
provided by the very gods.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  The divine blessings spell group consists of the following
spells:

prayer                  gives the priest the ability to use their blessings
holy flame              reveals others, and affects their armor
bind soul               weakens a target's ability to hit
mend wounds             provides healing energy to the target
cure deafness           cures the affliction of deafness
dispel curse            permanently removes the curse from an item
holy presence           boosts the target's armor defense
endurance               provides the target with greater health
mental drain            causes the target to find casting more difficult
faerie flames           reveals all those present, and affects their armor
displacement            returns the target involuntarily to their recall
divine intervention     affects a target's physical and mental abilities
enhanced recovery       increases the rate at which an individual recovers
nourishment             provides the target with food and drink nourishment
meteo                   calls down a wrath of meteors from the heavens

This group is available to the following classes -

     PRIEST
