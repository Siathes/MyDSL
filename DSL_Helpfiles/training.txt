        How to Train and Practice

    Type skill and spell to see a list of skills and spells you
      currently have access to. Type stat or score to see
      information on your stats.
    If you are at a trainer, type train (stat) to use one of your
      train points to increase a stat. Type practice (skill or spell
      name) to use a practice point to increase that skill or spell's
      efficiency. You can only practice a skill or spell to 75%. Skill's
      and spell's efficiency increases the more you use them.

    Stats:
      Strength - Affects physical damage and maximum weight capacity.
      Dexterity - Affects accuracy, evasion, and number of items carried.
      Wisdom - Affects resistence to spells and the practice points gained
        per level.
      Intelligence - Affects effectiveness with spells.
      Constitution - Affects how many hit points are gained per level.

    The general trainer in Althainia is located north and up from recall.

        Type HELP NEWBIE to return to the table of contents.
