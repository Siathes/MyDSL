SHARPWEAPON BLUNTWEAPON ARMORCRAFT

These third tier crafts require the use of all three types of refined
materials to create the finished product.  Depending on the item you are
trying to produce, it may take one board, bar and square of a particular
material or it many require several of each.  The more advanced you
become, the better your skill in working with the materials which will
result in fewer failed attempts and ruined supplies. For those adept in
their craft, it is rumored that on the rare occasion they can make
materials come together in such a way as to create the perfect item.

     Syntax: craft <skill> <item>

Weaponsmiths ( Sharp ) have the ability to craft sharp weaponry such
as swords, daggers, axes, spears and polearms.

     Example: craft sharpweapon 'long bronze trinket'

Weaponsmiths ( Blunt ) have the ability to craft blunt weaponry such
as maces, staffs, flails, whips and other exotic type weapons.

     Example: craft bluntweapon 'round bronze trinket'

Armorcrafters have the ability to create some of the armor that can be
worn though speciallizes in metal working to included studded leather,
chainmail and platemail pieces.

     Example: craft armorcraft 'bronze studs trinket'

If you are not skilled enough to create a certain item, you should get
a message that informs you of such as well as the needed skill level.

     Example:
              craft sharpweapon 'bronze dirk'
              You are not skilled enough to craft that.  Requires 22.
