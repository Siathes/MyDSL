leonine
SEE Help Wemics, Help Felar
Leonine are a race of cat-like people who live amongst Tropica in tribes
known as "prides".  They live fairly primitive, hunting their food almost
daily and defending their territory with great fury and pride.
Classes and Reclasses: Wemics can be Warriors or Clerics with the following
reclasses available.
Barbarian, Ranger, Druid, Shaman.
Felar can be Thieves, Warriors, Clerics or Mages with the following
reclasses:
Barbarian, Ranger, Assassin, Witch, Illusionists, Enchantors, Druid, or
Shaman.  (Conclave reclasses if in Conclave as well)
