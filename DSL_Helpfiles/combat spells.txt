'COMBAT SPELLS'

These sorcerous spells are, in the words of one famous mage, just 'more ways
to toss energy around'.  They are the most powerful of the damaging spells,
and considered an essential part of most wizards' collections.  Clerics and
thieves do not have access to these spells.

More information on individual spells may be found by typing help
(spellname).  In order of power, the combat spell group consists of the
following spells:

magic missile    sends a weak energy bolt into your foe.
chill touch      weakens your enemy with a frigid grasp.
burning hands    sends a blast of fire into your foe.
shocking grasp   sends a powerful jolt into your foe.
color spray      blasts your foe with a rainbow spray, which may blind them.
fireball         a powerful spell, great for burning your enemy to ashes.
blizzra          a chilling blizzard storms quickly over your foe.
acid blast       sends forth a stream of acid to eradicate your foe.
chain lightning  sends lightning bolts arcing through your foes.

This group is available to the following classes -
    ASSASSIN BATTLERAGER BLADESINGER DRAGON GIANT ILLUSIONIST
    INVOKER MAGE NECROMANCER RANGER THIEF TRANSMUTER WARRIOR
