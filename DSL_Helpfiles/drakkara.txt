DRAKKARA

Legends have it that Drakkara was born of the dark moons, having no
traditional parents, she simply came into being.  Necrucifer is her husband,
and together they rule the underside of the heavens, constantly scheming to
take over the land with their magicks and manipulation.

When she appears she does so with the utmost fanfare.  Being incredibly
beautiful, her almond shaped eyes can captivate even the most unwilling of
servants.  She has been known to dip her hand and meddle in the affairs of
the mortals, rewarding them greatly when they serve her.

Drakkara has ultimate control over dark magic and absolutely adores people
who pray to her.  Characteristically arrogant and pompous, she takes the
utmost care of herself, prolonging everything she wishes with her control
over magic.  She loves to entice mortal men and carry them to their deaths,
all under the guise of a farm-girl or other innocent.
