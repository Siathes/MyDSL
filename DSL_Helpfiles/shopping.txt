        Shopping and Shop Locations

    If you're at a vendor, you can take a look at his wares by typing
      list. Buy a listed item by typing buy (item name). You can
      attempt to sell your items by typing sell (item). If you want
      to buy multiples of an item, you can type buy #*(item). So if
      you wanted to buy 20 pot pies, type buy 20*pie.

    All of these directions are from Althainia recall. Keep in mind there
      are many shops in Algoron, but these are a key few that will be
      helpful for now.
        Bakery - 2s, w, n
        Grunting Boar - s, 2e
        General Store - 2s, e, n
        Armorsmith - 2s, w, s
        Weaponsmith - 2s, 2e, n
        Leather Shop - 3s, 2w, n
        Magic Shop - 2s, 2w, n
        Myth and Magic - 2s, 3w, 1n, w
        Blue Pixie Stable - 2s, 3e, s, e, n
        Mercenaries - 3s, 2e, n
        Melancholy's Maps - 3s, w, n

        Type HELP NEWBIE to return to the table of contents.
