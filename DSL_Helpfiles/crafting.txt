CRAFT CRAFTING

This is used by all top tier crafters to create items for player use.  You
may do this anywhere but you are required to have all the items needed for
the item you are trying to create.  For some crafts like weapon making you
will have a list of trinkets that you can craft to hone your skills.  You
can sell these trinkets to the merchants that sell knives, saws, and
pickaxes to cut your costs.  Additionally, vaults are essential to crafting
and can hold considerable amounts of material.  Shops are another asset
that can be utilized by the crafter to potentially sell their wares.

- You must be level 51 to be able to craft.  If you begin a craft at level
  51 and reclass, you need to wait until you hit level 51 again to resume
  your craft.

- Pay attention to craft weight or risk losing stock if you;re over burdened!

- You can only apprentice in two crafts at a time.  Most (not all) crafting
  room locations can register your apprenticeship for the craft you are
  selecting and will ask you if you wish to apprentice each time you enter.
  Typing apprentice alone will give you a list of all crafts currently
  available.  Type apprentice <selected craft> to register your crafting
  choice.  Example:

           apprentice blunt weapons
           You are now an apprentice of blunt weapons.

  If you wish to change or remove a craft, type apprentice <craft name> forget.

NOTE THAT IN DOING THIS, YOU WILL WIPE OUT ANYTHING PREVIOUSLY LEARNED
  INCLUDING ALL MONEY SPENT AND START OVER IN YOUR NEW CRAFT!!!!

- Each craft has a craftskill of 1 to 1000 and becomes more costly as you
  progress.  There are 11 Craft Ranks depending on the craftskill of the
  player:

            1  - 99  Helper
            100-199  Junior Apprentice
            200-299  Apprentice
            300-399  Neophyte
            400-499  Assistant
            500-599  Junior
            600-699  Journeyman
            700-799  Senior
            800-899  Master
            900-999  Grand Master
            1000+    Legendary Grand Master

- There are several tiers that apply to crafting.  Depending on the craft,
  there may be only one tier so an apprentice can work alone within that
  craft or as with other crafts, you are depeneant on items created from
  the other tiered apprentices in order for you to advance.

  1st tier crafters acquire the basic materials needed for crafting:

  Miners acquire ore           (Required: Pick Axes)
  Lumberjacks acquire logs     (Required: Saws)
  Hunters acquire animal skins (Required: Skinning Knives)

  2nd tier crafters refine the basic materials into usable items for 3rd tier:

  Smelters make metal bars from ore  (Required: Smelting Furnace)
  Millers make boards from logs      (Required: Enchanted Saw Mill)
  Tanners make leather squares skins (Required: Tanning Table)

  3rd tier crafters use materials from the world and from 2nd tier
  crafters to create weapons, armor, and various other equipment and
  items.  If there is only one tier in a craft it will be the 3rd tier.
  Current available 3rd tier crafts:

  Sharp Weapons, Blunt Weapons, Armor Crafting, Tailoring, Spellcrafting

  See each individual craft help file for more specific information.

THIRD TIER CRAFTING' '3rd TIER CRAFTING'

Apprentices of the 3rd tier create finished items such as weapons, armor
and other various equipment for player use.  Spellcrafting varies slightly
from the others and all are covered in separate help files. You may craft
anywhere but you are required to have all the items in your inventory
needed for the item you are trying to create.

All 3rd tier crafts require that you first master the making of a trinket
of a particular material or in the case of spellcrafting, a gemstone,
before you can create the weapons, armor or gems of that same material.
Every craft has a skill level range that you need to achieve in order
to created the items within that range.

You can sell all crafted trinkets back to the merchants that sell the
materials needed for any craft.  The price that you get for the trinkets
on resale depends on the type of material used.

Skills-

sharpweapon  - makes sharp weapons axes, swords, daggers, spears, and polearms
bluntweapon  - makes blunt weapons maces, flails, staffs, whips, and exotics
armorcraft   - makes leather studded, chainmail, and platemail armor
tailor       - makes cloth and leather armor
spellcrafter - makes gems to imbue onto armor and weapons

Note that if you have an alt that is a 1st or 2nd tier crafter and you
use  materials created by them for your alt that is a 3rd tier crafter, it
is illegal.  You cannot use material gained from one in order to benefit
the other.  See multiplaying rules.
