GAIN

The gain command is used to learn new skills, once the proper trainer has
been found.  The following options can be used with gain:

     gain list:  list all groups and skills that can be learned
   gain points:  lower your creation points by 1 (costs four training sessions)
    gain trade:  can only gain a trade in skills - not spells
     gain name:  add a skill or skill group, at the listed cost
 gain practice:  turns in 1 train for 5 practices
gain alignment:  changes a characters alignment, ethos, and deity

Gain uses training sessions, not practices. (see 'help train') Gained
skills and groups do NOT increase your experience per level or total
number of creation points and you can only trade in skills for other
skills not yet gained.

Skills from the rom basic/weaponsmaster and basic/default groups of your class
will not be tradable nor are skills that are inherent to your race.

The skill being traded in must be equal or less than the skill being traded
for.  You will NOT be reimbursed practices or trains.
