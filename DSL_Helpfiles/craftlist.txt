CRAFTLIST
This command is only useful for third tier crafters.  It will display
what items you can craft, the skill required to craft that item, and
the materials required to craft it.

Syntax: craftlist
        craftlist <item type>
        craftlist <minimum level> <maximum level>
        craftlist <minimum level> <maximum level> <item type>

Examples: If you are a sharp weaponsmith...
 craftlist          - lists everything you can craft. (Very long list.)
 craftlist trinket  - lists all the trinkets used to advance your skills.
 craftlist 1 10     - lists every item you can craft that is level 1 to 10.
 craftlist 1 10 axe - lists every axe you can craft that is level 1 to 10.

Valid <item types>:
axe, sword, dagger, spear, polearm, mace, exotic, flail, whip, staff,
cloth, leather, studded, chain, plate, obsidian, moonstone, opal,
amethyst, emerald, sapphire, ruby, diamond, and trinket
