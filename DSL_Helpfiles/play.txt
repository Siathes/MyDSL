PLAY
Syntax:  play list
         play <song>
This is a command that can only be used with jukeboxes.  You may use the
first form of the syntax to list the playable songs for a jukebox when you
are in the same room with one.  Use the second form of the syntax with enough
of the name of a song to be unique from the other playable songs to have the
jukebox start "playing" the song for you.  The lyrics of the song will only
be heard in the room that the jukebox is in.  The song will not be played over
the music channel.
So far, no attempt has been made to use Mud Sound Protocol with the jukeboxes
on DSL, but that is a possibility for the future.  For help with sound, see
SOUND.  For help with the music channel, see MUSIC.  Note that both SOUND and
MUSIC are totally unrelated to PLAY at this time (March 1999)
