WHOISOOC OOCSET
Syntax: whoisooc <character name>
Syntax: oocset <field> <value>
Field can be any of: 'name' 'city' 'state' 'nation' 'email' 'www'
        The tilde does not translate into WWW addresses, so use a {- instead,
this will place a tilde in its place.
The OOC, or out of character, who list is accessed with the
'whoisooc' command (see syntax below).
        Although DSL does not currently require you to fill in your OOC
information, you are highly urged to do so.  Because many players like to
keep their true identities hidden, you may mask parts of your data.  Your
game position and login time are not maskable, nor is your character's name,
but the rest of your data is.  The 'oocset' command is used to change
your OOC Wholist data, set pieces of data on and off, and view your current
hidden/visible status.
        Typing 'oocset' along will give you a list of your current
visibility/hidden status.  All new players start with no information, and all
data hidden.  To toggle a given field on, use 'oocset <field name>'.  For
your Location, any of city, state, or nation will turn it on or off.  Your
geographical location acts as one field for the sake of masked data.
        To set your OOC Wholist data, use 'oocset <field> <value>'.  For
example: 'oocset name Pat Migroin' will set your real life name to 'Pat
Migroin.'  Note that this is TRUE data, and if you wish not to use it, then
do NOT fill it in with bogus data.  You will be punished for it.
