VOICE TYPE

Syntax:  voicetype <number>

Players have the option of selecting and/or toggling between different
voice types in order to add ambience to their characters.

Please enter the number corresponding to the voice type you wish.

 1. Normal             2. Squeaky
 3. Raspy              4. Low toned
 5. High Pitched       6. Deep Voice
 7. Soft               8. Booming
 9. Stressed          10. Evil
11. Sweet             12. Demanding
13. Growlingly        14. Cheer
15. Sarcasm           16. Sad
17. Musical           18. Gruff
19. Breathy           20. Wheezy
21. Mumbling          22. Husky
