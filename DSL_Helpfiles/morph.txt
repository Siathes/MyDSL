morph
Syntax: morph
Members of the sea elf race have their strongest ties to nature through the
vast oceans of Algoron.  The close ties with the seas have required these
elves to spend more time within the oceans and hence find a way to survive
in them.  The sea elves have learned through their many years of close
ties with the oceans how to change their elvish form into one of the most
majestic sea creatures, the dolphin.  The dolphin form that the sea elves
morph into are rumored to be quite adept at survival and obviously are
unaffected by the affects of the rough seas which can take its toll on the
strongest mortal. To return to elven form use the command revert.
