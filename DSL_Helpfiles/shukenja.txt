SHUKENJA

The art of the Shukenja was developed in the lands of Shokono. The people
there held a deep devotion to their ancestors and the wisdom that was passed
down through the ages. It was believed that a strength could be gained if
one could speak to those spirits directly and use their power as his or her
own. Through these studies the Shukenja forms were developed and allowed one
to combine the power of their ancestors, healing magics and both single and
dual wielding weapon techniques.

Who can be a Shukenja?

CLASS:     CLERICS only
RACE:      ANY
ALIGNMENT: Any alignment
CLAN:      Any, including non-clanned
